Practice: hands-on payment operations

Reading about a payment is one thing. Running one, breaking it, investigating it and fixing it is how it sticks. These five practical experiences use synthetic educational data only. Nothing here connects to a real banking system and nothing moves real money. Your progress is stored in this browser only, and there is no sign-in and no payment to use any of it.

1. Mission ControlRun payment operations. Four live missions: a payment stuck in processing, a duplicate submission, a clearing rejection, and an API timeout. Investigate, decide, and see the outcome scored.Practise · 4 missions · Scored 2. Payment Failure Investigation LabA payment failed. Read the transaction record, the events and the error, find the likely root cause, pick the next investigation step, and compare with the reference solution.Practise · 7 failure types · Scored 3. Includes a pain.001 to pacs.008 exercise.Build · 7 messages · Guided exercise 4. Reconciliation War RoomMatch payment instructions, clearing records, settlement records and bank statement entries.
