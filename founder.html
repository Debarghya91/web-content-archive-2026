About the Founder

Debarghya Mondal

Debarghya Mondal is a Senior Business Analyst working in payments and transaction banking. He works at Capco in Bangalore, deployed to NatWest in the United Kingdom, where he has worked on payments transformation since December 2021.

Experience

He has 8.5 years of experience as a business analyst in banking and payments, and 11.5 years of experience in total. Before Capco he worked at Cognizant, deployed to BNY Mellon in the United States, and at Standard Chartered. He started his career as a Java developer at Cognizant.

His work covers core banking, business portfolios and product structures, and aligning payment products and processes with regulatory frameworks. The subjects on this site are the subjects he works with every day: ISO 20022 and SWIFT messaging, domestic and cross-border payment systems, clearing and settlement, cards, APIs, and the operations that keep payments running.

Education

He holds a Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGPM, MBA) from Great Lakes Institute of Management, completed in 2018, and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology, completed in 2013.

Why this site exists

Payments Training Hub exists because payments are taught badly in most places. The material is either too shallow to use on a real project, or it is a rulebook that nobody can read. This site teaches payments the way banks actually run them: the messages, the systems, the failure modes, and the operational consequences, worked through with real examples.

The site is written by one person, not a faculty. There is no team of instructors, no guest faculty, and no certification attached to the content. What is here is what the founder knows from working in payments, checked against the scheme documentation listed on the Sources page.

Contact

The way to reach the founder is through the Contact page. Corrections, questions about a lesson, and suggestions for new topics are all welcome.
