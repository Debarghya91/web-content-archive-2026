Introduction to Payments for Aspiring Business Analysts

A free live session that shows you how payments really work, and what a Business Analyst actually does with them. One hour, plain language, real examples from banking work.

Session details

  • Date: Saturday 28 November 2026
  • Time: 6:00 to 7:00 PM IST
  • Duration: 1 hour
  • Price: Free

Who it is for. Aspiring Business Analysts moving into the payments domain, with up to 5 years of experience. No payments background needed.

What you will take away

  • How a payment moves end to end. Follow one payment from the customer to the beneficiary, through every system in between, so you can explain the journey with confidence.
  • The messages that matter. Read a pacs.008 instruction and a pacs.002 status report, and learn what each one proves about a payment.
  • What a payments BA actually does. The daily work: writing requirements, mapping message flows, and running workshops with operations and engineering teams.
  • Exceptions and investigations. How a failed payment is traced on evidence, and the checks that find the cause fast.
  • Preparing for payments interviews. The questions interviewers ask, and how to answer them with real examples instead of memorised definitions.
