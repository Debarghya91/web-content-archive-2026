What the test player includes
  • Section tabs follow the exam's own section order. Sections with a time limit count down separately and move you on automatically when the limit ends.
  • The question palette shows answered, not answered, marked for review, answered and marked, and not visited questions in different colours.
  • Mark for review, clear response, previous and next, and an on-screen calculator are always available.
  • Submit only after the confirmation screen, which lists how many questions you answered, left blank and marked in each section.
  • Results show your total score under the mock's own marking scheme, section-wise score and accuracy, time spent per question, and the correct answer and explanation for every question.
