Instructions

How to use this test screen

  • Pick an option to answer a multiple choice question.
  • Save and Next saves your answer and moves to the next question. Mark for Review and Next marks the question and moves on.
  • Previous goes back to the previous question where your exam allows it. Some exams do not allow going back.
  • Clear Response removes your answer for this question.
  • The palette shows answered, not answered, marked and not visited questions. Tap a palette number to jump where your exam allows it.
  • Hide clock hides the section timer; the clock keeps running. This control follows the 2011 demo; the current console has not confirmed it.
  • Question Paper lists every question. Tap Go to open a question where your exam allows it.
  • Instructions shows this exam's rules again.
  • Scratchpad is rough work for the current question and is never scored.
  • Submit Test ends the test. A summary appears first so you can check what is answered.
