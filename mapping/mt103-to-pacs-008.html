MT103 to pacs.008 Field Mapping

Field by field mapping from MT103 to pacs.008.001.10: 24 canonical rows merged from the MT to MX field mapping workbook (15 rows) and the ISO 20022 interface mapping CSV (18 rows).

Field :54a: has no direct element

Field :54a:, the receiver's correspondent, has no dedicated MX element. Where settlement information for the receiver's correspondent exists, it is carried in the settlement block (SttlmInf), for example as the settlement account (SttlmAcct), rather than as a separate party element.

Reconciliation notes

Where the workbook and the CSV disagreed, the canonical mapping follows the resolution below. Neither source is globally authoritative.

  • :23B: to PmtTpInf/SvcLvl/Cd: CSV authoritative. XLSX 'No direct element' is WRONG: 23B CRED maps to SvcLvl Cd G001. The transfer type is not conveyed by the message type alone; the service level code is the MX carrier for the bank operation code.
  • :71A: to ChrgBr: XLSX authoritative: OUR->DEBT, BEN->CRED, SHA->SHAR. CSV is WRONG twice over: its instruction to 'map BEN OUR SHA unchanged to ChrgBr' would emit values that are not valid ChargeBearerType1Code values, and its note ('DEBT and CRED are not valid in pacs.008') is inverted - DEBT/CRED/SHAR (plus SLEV, FollowingServiceLevel, for scheme rulebooks) are exactly the valid ChrgBr values. A fourth value, SLEV, has no MT :71A: equivalent.
