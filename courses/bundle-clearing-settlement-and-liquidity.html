Clearing, Settlement and Liquidity

Clearing is not settlement. Learn the difference properly.

The plumbing underneath every payment. Clearing concepts and netting, settlement models, liquidity management and settlement risk. Short, focused, and the difference between a BA who guesses and one who knows. It also covers settlement design and failure, clearing and settlement mechanisms, and how clearing systems work.

7 chapters · 49 lessons · Pricing to be announced

Estimated duration: 3.9 hours of narration, plus exercises, simulators and tests.

Who this course is for

Business analysts, treasury staff and operations leads who need to understand settlement.

Prerequisites

Payment Foundations, or equivalent working knowledge of the payment lifecycle.

What you will learn

  • Explain clearing and netting.
  • Compare RTGS, deferred net settlement and prefunded models.
  • Explain liquidity management and intraday credit.
  • Explain settlement risk and how it is controlled.
  • Calculate a net settlement position.
  • Design a settlement system and explain how clearing systems work, including when settlement fails.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 49 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A net settlement position calculation.
  • A settlement model comparison for one payment system.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the bundle.
  • Pass the Clearing and Settlement Test with 70% or more.
