Group 1: Payments Foundations

What payments are, and how they move.

Start here. What a payment is, payment instruments and types, the payment ecosystem, the end-to-end lifecycle, cheques and paper instruments, what money is, and accounts and balances. For students, new joiners, career switchers, and anyone starting from zero. After this course you speak the language of payments and can follow any payment from initiation to settlement. How money actually moves in each market. India (UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS), US (Fedwire, FedNow, ACH, CHIPS), Europe (SEPA and T2), UK (Faster Payments, CHAPS, BACS), TARGET services, TIPS in depth, SEPA deep dives, global instant systems, and the UK, US and SEPA systems in depth. After this course you can read a scheme rulebook, follow a payment on any major rail, and compare rails to recommend the right one.

Who this group is for

Students, new joiners, career switchers, and anyone starting from zero. BAs, testers and product owners joining a market-specific project in India, the US, Europe or the UK.

Prerequisites

None. Course 1, or equivalent working knowledge of what a payment is.

What you will learn

  • Explain what a payment is and how it differs from a transfer and from settlement.
  • Name the payment instruments and when each is used.
  • Describe the payment ecosystem and the parties in it.
  • Trace a payment through its lifecycle end to end.
  • Explain what money is, how accounts hold balances, and how cheques and paper instruments still work.
  • Explain how UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS work in India.
  • Explain Fedwire, FedNow, ACH and CHIPS in the US.
  • Explain SEPA, T2, T2S and TIPS in Europe.
  • Explain Faster Payments, CHAPS and BACS in the UK.
  • Compare real-time payment systems across markets and recommend a rail for a corridor.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This group has 186 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A written walkthrough of one payment from instruction to beneficiary credit.
  • A rails comparison for one payment corridor, naming the system, the settlement model and the cut-off.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the group.
