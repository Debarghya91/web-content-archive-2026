Flagship

The flagship. Everything in the academy, all 15 domains.

The complete Payments Training Hub academy in one course: all 100 chapters and all 746 narrated lessons, from what a payment is to payment hub architecture, ISO 20022 and SWIFT messaging, clearing and settlement, cards, compliance, APIs, exceptions, reconciliation and the BA and PO professional practice. Buy this and every chapter unlocks.

100 chapters · 746 lessons · ₹9,999

Estimated duration: 55.5 hours of narration, plus exercises, simulators and tests.

Who this course is for

Anyone who wants the complete payments education: business analysts, product owners, engineers, operations leads and career changers moving into payments.

Prerequisites

None. The course starts from first principles and builds up. The Beginner pathway is the recommended route through it.

What you will learn

  • Explain what a payment is and trace it from instruction to beneficiary credit.
  • Read and write ISO 20022 and SWIFT messages field by field.
  • Design payment APIs and integration requirements.
  • Investigate exceptions, returns and recalls.
  • Run reconciliation and operational controls.
  • Compare settlement models and recommend one for a bank.
  • Design a payment hub architecture.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 746 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • The capstone project (ch29) with a full business analysis deliverable.
  • An integration specification (OpenAPI contract, message mapping, test pack).
  • A settlement model comparison and recommendation.
  • A payment hub architecture document.
  • A product requirement document for a new payment feature.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all 746 lessons.
  • Pass all 12 domain tests with 70% or more.
  • Complete the required simulator and lab exercises.
  • Submit the capstone project.
