Compliance, Risk, Operations and Reconciliation

Sanctions, AML, exceptions, returns and the daily ops grind.

The control and operations bundle. Sanctions screening, AML, KYC and fraud prevention, payment security, payment reconciliation, payment exceptions and investigations, payment operations, reject and return in CBPR+, and payment operations architecture. It also covers claims, enquiries and case management, GDPR, BSA/AML and operational resilience, regulatory compliance in payments, the controls that keep payments safe, and screening, fraud and sanctions operations in detail.

13 chapters · 98 lessons · ₹1,999

Estimated duration: 7.8 hours of narration, plus exercises, simulators and tests.

Available for purchase in the Payments Training Hub iOS app.

← Back to all courses

Who this course is for

Compliance, risk, operations and reconciliation staff in payments.

Prerequisites

Payment Foundations, or equivalent working knowledge of the payment lifecycle.

What you will learn

  • Explain sanctions screening and name matching.
  • Explain AML, KYC and fraud prevention, including APP fraud and the UK PSR reimbursement rules.
  • Explain payment security: OAuth2, mTLS and API security.
  • Reconcile a payment against its statement and find the break.
  • Investigate a payment exception to its resolution.
  • Communicate an incident to the people who need to know.
  • Handle claims, enquiries and case management, apply GDPR and BSA/AML rules, and run screening, fraud and sanctions operations.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 98 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A reconciliation break investigation.
  • An exception investigation write-up.
  • An incident communication for a real scenario.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the bundle.
  • Pass the Compliance and Risk Test and the Operations Test with 70% or more.
  • Complete the Reconciliation War Room and Failure Investigation Lab.
