Payment Architecture and APIs

Hubs, platforms, gateways and integration, the engineering view.

The architecture bundle. Payment hub architecture, API banking and open banking, payment gateway architecture, payment platform architecture, system design deep dive, payment data model and mapping, the payment hub enterprise architecture, and open banking, PSD2 and PSD3. It also covers payment engine internals and observability, and API design standards and integration patterns.

10 chapters · 76 lessons · ₹2,499

Estimated duration: 6.3 hours of narration, plus exercises, simulators and tests.

Who this course is for

Solution architects, engineers and technical business analysts designing payment platforms.

Prerequisites

ISO 20022 and SWIFT Messaging, or equivalent technical payments knowledge.

What you will learn

  • Design a payment hub architecture.
  • Design a payment API contract in OpenAPI, including idempotency and error handling.
  • Explain event-driven payment architecture and Kafka.
  • Design for resilience and disaster recovery.
  • Explain cloud and deployment architecture for payment platforms.
  • Explain OAuth2 and mTLS for payment APIs.
  • Explain payment engine internals and observability, and design APIs to recognised standards.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 76 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A payment hub architecture document.
  • An OpenAPI contract for a payment API.
  • A resilience and disaster recovery design.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the bundle.
  • Pass the Architecture Test and the APIs Test with 70% or more.
  • Complete the Hub Architecture Simulator and API Playground exercises.
