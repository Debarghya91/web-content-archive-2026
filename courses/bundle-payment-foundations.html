Payment Foundations

What a payment is, and how the ecosystem fits together.

Start here. What a payment is, payment instruments and types, the payment ecosystem, the end-to-end lifecycle, and cheques and paper instruments. For anyone new to payments who wants the ground floor solid before touching messages or rails.

5 chapters · 37 lessons · ₹1,499

Estimated duration: 2.6 hours of narration, plus exercises, simulators and tests.

Who this course is for

Anyone new to payments, with no banking background.

Prerequisites

None.

What you will learn

  • Explain what a payment is and how it differs from a transfer and from settlement.
  • Name the payment instruments and when each is used.
  • Describe the payment ecosystem and the parties in it.
  • Trace a payment through its lifecycle end to end.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 37 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A written walkthrough of one payment from instruction to beneficiary credit.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the bundle.
  • Pass the Foundations Test with 70% or more.
  • Submit the capstone walkthrough.
