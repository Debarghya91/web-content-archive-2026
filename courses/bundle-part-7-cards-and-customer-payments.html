Who this course is for

Cards product staff, acquiring BAs, and wallet and POS project teams.

Prerequisites

Course 1, or equivalent working knowledge of the payment lifecycle.

What you will learn

  • Explain card authorisation, clearing and settlement.
  • Explain 3D Secure, tokenisation and chargebacks.
  • Compare Visa and Mastercard network rules.
  • Explain digital wallets, POS systems, recurring payments and 3RI.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 37 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A card transaction walkthrough from authorisation to settlement, naming each party and each fee.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the course.
  • Pass the course tests with 70% or more.
