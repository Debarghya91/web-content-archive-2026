ISO 20022 and SWIFT Messaging

MT and MX messages, field by field, with real examples.

The messaging bundle. SWIFT MT messages, ISO 20022 fundamentals, the pacs.008 deep dive, MT to MX migration and CBPR+, the complete ISO 20022 catalogue, MT101, SWIFT reporting messages, MT103 optional fields, SWIFT fundamentals, pain.001 and pain.002, and ISO 20022 technical implementation.

11 chapters · 87 lessons · Pricing to be announced

Estimated duration: 6.8 hours of narration, plus exercises, simulators and tests.

Who this course is for

Business analysts, engineers and operations staff who work with payment messages every day.

Prerequisites

Payment Foundations, or equivalent working knowledge of what a payment is.

What you will learn

  • Read SWIFT MT messages field by field.
  • Read and write ISO 20022 messages (pacs.008, pain.001, pain.002, camt.052, camt.053, camt.054, camt.060).
  • Map MT messages to ISO 20022.
  • Validate an ISO 20022 XML file against its schema.
  • Explain CBPR+ migration and what it changes.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 87 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A message mapping for one payment type.
  • A validated ISO 20022 XML file with the errors found and fixed.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the bundle.
  • Pass the Messaging Test with 70% or more.
  • Complete the ISO Lab exercises.
