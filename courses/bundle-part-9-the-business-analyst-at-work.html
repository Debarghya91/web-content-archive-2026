Part 9: The Business Analyst at Work

Requirements, stories, BRDs, workshops, AI, and the capstone.

The BA part. The BA practical workshop, payment product management, product strategy and business models, scheme onboarding, market expansion and build versus buy, BA practice depth, the capstone project, AI foundations for the payments BA and PO, AI-assisted BA work, AI product ownership, and AI in payments operations and capstone. After this part you can do the BA job end to end: gather requirements, write the BRD and user stories, map messages, run workshops, use AI properly, and deliver the capstone.

Who this course is for

Working and aspiring business analysts, product owners and product managers.

Prerequisites

Part 1 and Part 3, or equivalent.

What you will learn

  • Gather requirements and write BRDs, FSDs and Jira user stories for payments changes.
  • Map messages, run workshops, and write test cases for a payments project.
  • Use AI to draft BA artifacts, and validate what it produces.
  • Own an AI product: define the use case, the data and model requirements, the guardrails and the metrics.
  • Deliver the capstone: a full business analysis deliverable for a payments change.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 79 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A BRD and FSD for a payments change, AI-assisted and human-validated.
  • A set of Jira user stories for a payments feature, with acceptance criteria.
  • The capstone project (Book chapter 92) with a full business analysis deliverable.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the part.
  • Pass the part tests with 70% or more.
  • Submit the capstone project.
