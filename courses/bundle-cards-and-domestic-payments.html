Cards and Domestic Payments

Authorisation, clearing, settlement, wallets and POS.

The card bundle. Card authorisation, clearing and settlement, 3D Secure, tokenization and chargebacks, card networks (Visa and Mastercard), digital wallets and POS systems, recurring payments and 3RI, and the card payments deep dive. It also covers cards compliance and acquiring.

7 chapters · 52 lessons · Pricing to be announced

Estimated duration: 3.7 hours of narration, plus exercises, simulators and tests.

Who this course is for

Business analysts and product owners working on card payments.

Prerequisites

Payment Foundations, or equivalent working knowledge of the payment lifecycle.

What you will learn

  • Explain card authorisation, clearing and settlement.
  • Explain 3D Secure, tokenisation and chargebacks.
  • Compare Visa and Mastercard network rules.
  • Explain digital wallets and POS systems.
  • Explain recurring payments and 3RI.
  • Explain cards compliance and acquiring.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 52 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A card transaction walkthrough from authorisation to settlement, naming each party and each fee.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the bundle.
  • Pass the Cards Test with 70% or more.
