Cross-Border and Correspondent Banking

How international payments really work, end to end.

The cross-border bundle. Correspondent banking and nostro/vostro, serial versus cover payments, charges, FX and gpi tracking, cross-border exceptions, standby letters of credit, documentary collections and SWIFT category 4, and trade finance.

7 chapters · 50 lessons · Pricing to be announced

Estimated duration: 3.0 hours of narration, plus exercises, simulators and tests.

Who this course is for

Business analysts and operations staff working on cross-border payments.

Prerequisites

Payment Foundations and ISO 20022 and SWIFT Messaging, or equivalent.

What you will learn

  • Explain correspondent banking and nostro/vostro accounts.
  • Compare serial and cover payments and choose between them.
  • Calculate what a beneficiary receives under OUR, SHA and BEN.
  • Explain FX rates, spreads and conversion.
  • Track a payment with SWIFT gpi and UETR.
  • Handle cross-border exceptions, rejects and returns.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 50 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A worked OUR/SHA/BEN charge calculation for a real corridor.
  • A cross-border exception investigation write-up.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the bundle.
  • Pass the Cross-Border Test with 70% or more.
