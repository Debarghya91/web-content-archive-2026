Course 5: Clearing, Settlement, Liquidity and Reconciliation

Why take this course

Clearing is not settlement. Learn the difference properly. The settlement part. You will be able to: Explain clearing and netting, and compare RTGS, deferred net settlement and prefunded models; Explain liquidity management, intraday credit and settlement risk; Reconcile a payment against its statement and find the break; Explain ledger posting, cash management, and how clearing systems work, including when settlement fails; Calculate a net settlement position.

Clearing is not settlement. Learn the difference properly.

The settlement course. Clearing concepts and netting, settlement models, card authorisation, clearing and settlement, ledger posting and reconciliation, ledger systems inside banks, liquidity management, settlement risk, payment reconciliation, payment operations, cash management, settlement design and failure, clearing and settlement mechanisms, and how clearing systems work. Who this course is for

Settlement, reconciliation, liquidity and operations staff, and BAs on back-office and ledger projects.

Prerequisites

Course 1, or equivalent working knowledge of the payment lifecycle.

What you will learn

  • Explain clearing and netting, and compare RTGS, deferred net settlement and prefunded models.
  • Explain liquidity management, intraday credit and settlement risk.
  • Reconcile a payment against its statement and find the break.
  • Explain ledger posting, cash management, and how clearing systems work, including when settlement fails.
  • Calculate a net settlement position.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This course has 110 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A net settlement position calculation.
  • A settlement model comparison for one payment system.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the course.
  • Pass the course tests with 70% or more.
