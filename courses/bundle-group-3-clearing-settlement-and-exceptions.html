Group 3: Clearing, Settlement and Exceptions

Clearing, settlement, liquidity, exceptions, and investigations.

The settlement course. Clearing concepts and netting, settlement models, card authorisation, clearing and settlement, ledger posting and reconciliation, ledger systems inside banks, liquidity management, settlement risk, payment reconciliation, payment operations, cash management, settlement design and failure, clearing and settlement mechanisms, and how clearing systems work. After this course you can run the settlement day: clear, net, settle, fund liquidity, reconcile the books, and clear breaks. The controls course. Lifecycle timelines and failure modes, sanctions screening, AML, KYC and fraud prevention, payment exceptions and investigations, regulatory compliance, controls that keep payments safe, screening, fraud and sanctions operations in detail, claims, enquiries and case management, cards compliance and acquiring, GDPR, BSA/AML and operational resilience, and AI governance, risk and control. After this course you can run an investigation to closure, clear a sanctions alert, handle a return or recall, and prove the control worked.

Who this group is for

Settlement, reconciliation, liquidity and operations staff, and BAs on back-office and ledger projects. Investigations analysts, exceptions teams, compliance, fraud and risk staff, and BAs on control projects.

Prerequisites

Course 1, or equivalent working knowledge of the payment lifecycle.

What you will learn

  • Explain clearing and netting, and compare RTGS, deferred net settlement and prefunded models.
  • Explain liquidity management, intraday credit and settlement risk.
  • Reconcile a payment against its statement and find the break.
  • Explain ledger posting, cash management, and how clearing systems work, including when settlement fails.
  • Calculate a net settlement position.
  • Explain sanctions screening, name matching, AML, KYC and fraud prevention.
  • Investigate a payment exception to its resolution and handle returns, recalls and claims.
  • Explain payment security, GDPR, BSA/AML rules and operational resilience.
  • Run screening, fraud and sanctions operations, and apply the AI governance control framework.
  • Communicate an incident to the people who need to know.

Practical exercises

Every lesson includes a practical exercise in the lesson player, with the problem, the information available, the action expected, what counts as a correct result, and why the answer is correct. This group has 208 exercises, one per lesson, plus the hands-on labs and simulators listed below.

What you will produce

  • A net settlement position calculation.
  • A settlement model comparison for one payment system.
  • A reconciliation break investigation.
  • An exception investigation write-up.
  • An incident communication for a real scenario.

Completion criteria

  • Finish all lessons in the group.
