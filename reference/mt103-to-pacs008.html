MT103 to pacs.008: Field-by-Field Reference

Every field of the SWIFT MT103 single customer credit transfer, and where it lands in the ISO 20022 pacs.008 FIToFICustomerCreditTransfer. This is the reference a payments analyst uses when reading a legacy message, building a mapping, or checking a translation.

The two messages

MT103 is the SWIFT FIN message for a single customer credit transfer: one payment per message, in numbered fields. pacs.008 is its ISO 20022 successor: the FIToFICustomerCreditTransfer, which carries one or many payments in structured XML. Since the CBPR+ coexistence period ended on 22 November 2025, pacs.008 is the standard format for cross-border customer credit transfers on SWIFT. A single payment typically starts life as a pain.001 from the customer and becomes a pacs.008 in the interbank leg.

The 32A and 33B distinction

This is the distinction that causes the most errors. Field 32A is the value date, the currency and the amount settled between the banks: it maps to IntrBkSttlmAmt, the interbank settlement amount, and IntrBkSttlmDt, the interbank settlement date. Field 33B is the currency and amount the ordering customer instructed: it maps to InstdAmt, the instructed amount. When a payment is converted, 32A and 33B differ, and the difference is the conversion. When there is no conversion, they carry the same amount. Never read 32A as the instructed amount.

What pacs.008 adds that MT103 does not have

  • Batching. One pacs.008 can carry many payments, each in its own CdtTrfTxInf block with its own UETR. MT103 carries one payment per message.
  • Structured parties. Names up to 140 characters, structured addresses, LEIs and other identifiers, instead of four 35-character lines.
  • More agents. pacs.008 has dedicated elements for up to three intermediary agents and three previous instructing agents, and for the instructing, instructed and third reimbursement agents.
  • EndToEndId. A reference set by the originator that must pass through unchanged, so the payer can recognise the payment on their statement.
  • Settlement method. GrpHdr / SttlmInf / SttlmMtd states how the payment settles: INDA or INGA (settled by an agent), COVE (covered by a separate pacs.009), or CLRG (through a clearing system).
  • Richer purpose. PmtTpInf carries service level, local instrument and category purpose as separate elements, instead of the single instruction and transaction type codes.

How to use this reference

Use this table as a field-by-field guide, not as a translator configuration. Mappings follow the CBPR+ usage guidelines, and translators vary: validate against the applicable usage guideline and your translator version before production use. When a pacs.008 is converted back down to an MT103 for a bank still on FIN, the extra detail has nowhere to go and is truncated, which is a common source of repair queues. Check the current SWIFT CBPR+ usage guidelines and the ISO 20022 message definition for pacs.008.001.08 before relying on any single mapping.
