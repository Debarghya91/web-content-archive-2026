Free vs paid

Free first lessons, no purchase needed

The first lesson of every chapter is free to read in full, and every other lesson shows what to expect from the lesson, with the option to buy its course. Buying a course unlocks the full lessons.

What buying unlocks

Smaller bundles cover one area each. A bundle unlocks only its own chapters, so pick the area you need, or take everything with the integrated course. The integrated course is the whole academy.
