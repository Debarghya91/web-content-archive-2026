Checkout: enrol and pay

Enrol by UPI, paid by hand. This is not an automatic checkout. You pay by UPI yourself using the QR code or the UPI button below, then submit your payment reference (UTR) on this page. The owner checks each payment by hand against its UTR and grants access manually. Payment Situations costs ₹4,999. Every other course shows "Pricing to be announced" and has no payment step until the owner sets its price: no amount is shown and no payment is taken. All lessons are free to read right now: no payment is needed for any lesson, and nothing is locked while enrolment is being set up.

How you can pay

  • UPI. Working now: pay to the UPI ID below and submit your UTR.
  • Card. Not switched on yet: no card payment is taken on this page.
  • Net banking. Not switched on yet: no net banking payment is taken on this page.
  • Wallet. Not switched on yet: no wallet payment is taken on this page.

Whichever method you pick, the payment itself goes through UPI for now, and the owner checks it by hand.

After paying, submit your payment reference here. The owner checks it against the payment received and grants access. Keep your UTR safe: it is the proof of your payment.

Payment deducted but access not granted yet?

If the money left your account but your access has not changed, keep your UTR and your claim id: they are the proof of your payment. Then re-check the claim status on this page, in the purchase status above and in your submitted payments below. Verification is manual by the owner, so the status changes only after the owner has checked your payment against its UTR.
