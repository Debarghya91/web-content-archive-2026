Payment Systems Training

Every payment settles through a system, and every system has its own rules: its own hours, its own settlement model, its own message formats and its own failure modes. This track teaches the domestic payment systems a payments professional meets most: the US, Europe, the UK and India, plus the instant systems now running worldwide. You will learn to tell RTGS from ACH from instant, to explain when each one settles, and to name what breaks when a system goes down.

What you will learn

  • How RTGS, ACH and instant systems differ in timing, settlement and risk.
  • US systems: Fedwire, FedNow, ACH and CHIPS, and when each one is used.
  • Europe: SEPA credit transfer and direct debit, T2, TIPS and TARGET services.
  • UK systems: Faster Payments, CHAPS and BACS, and the BACS three-day cycle.
  • India: UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS, and how NACH, BBPS and FEMA fit in.
  • Global instant systems, and what 24/7 settlement changes for operations.

Chapters in this track

  • India: UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS (9 lessons). How India moves money: instant, batch and real-time side by side.
  • US: Fedwire, FedNow, ACH, CHIPS (12 lessons). The four US systems, and which payment goes to which one.
  • Europe: SEPA and T2 (12 lessons). SEPA schemes and T2 settlement for the euro area.
  • UK: Faster Payments, CHAPS, BACS (8 lessons). Faster Payments, CHAPS and BACS: speed, value and the three-day cycle.
  • TARGET Services: T2, T2S and TIPS (8 lessons). T2, T2S and TIPS: how euro payments settle in central bank money.
  • TIPS in Depth: Instant Payments and Liquidity Transfers (8 lessons). TIPS in depth: instant settlement and liquidity transfers.
  • Global instant systems (18 lessons). Instant systems around the world, and what always-on settlement demands.
  • UK payment systems in depth (9 lessons). UK systems in depth, for BAs working on UK programmes.

These chapters sit in Group 1: Payments Foundations, with 21 chapters and 186 lessons in total.
