Course Map

The full Payments Training Hub course covers 100 chapters, 881 lessons, 564 payment situations, 320 interview questions and 10 simulators. Use this page to plan your route: pick a role pathway below, or browse the full chapter index grouped by domain.

For the chapter-by-chapter view of which course covers each chapter, open the curriculum index.

How to use this map

Each pathway lists the chapters it covers, what you will be able to do when you finish it, and the real narration time. Narration time is the total length of the lesson audio; practice and exercises take longer, at your own pace. The first lesson of every chapter is free to read in full, and every other lesson shows what to expect from the lesson, with the option to buy its course. Courses can be bought on the payment page with UPI, or in the app. The prices shown are the current launch offer prices.

Payment Scenarios: A Reading Path Through 26 Cases

Twenty-six payment scenarios as a reading path over the existing curriculum. Twenty-three stops follow the SC scenario records, from serial and cover routing decisions through RMA, SSI selection, UETR tracking, instant payment timeouts, UPI failures, chargebacks and direct debit returns. Three further stops cover the standard subjects every scenario reader needs: the MT210 nostro funding advice, the OUR, SHA and BEN charge cases, and the ACK and NACK acknowledgement flows. Each stop names its scenario and points at the lessons that explain it; no scenario content is invented.

Who it is for: Working payments professionals and business analysts who want to learn through scenarios: one case at a time, each grounded in the lessons that explain it.
