Course Map

The full Payments Training Hub course covers 100 chapters, 767 lessons, 564 payment situations, 320 interview questions and 10 simulators. Use this page to plan your route: pick a role pathway below, or browse the full chapter index grouped by domain.

How to use this map

Each pathway lists the chapters it covers, what you will be able to do when you finish it, and the estimated study time. Estimated at 12 minutes per lesson for narration plus practice. Lessons are free to read on this website. Buy on this website through checkout, or in the Payments Training Hub iOS app. Website checkout is being set up and is not taking live payments yet.

Cross-Border Payments

Cross-border payments from the ground up to professional depth: correspondent banking and nostro/vostro, the serial and cover methods with the exact SWIFT and ISO 20022 messages at every step, charges and FX, gpi tracking, exceptions and returns, corporate MT101 payments, documentary collections and MT400, standby letters of credit, and the SEPA, UK and US schemes the payment travels through.
