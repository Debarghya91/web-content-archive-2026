About Payments Training Hub

Payments Training Hub is an independent education resource for people who work with payments: business analysts, product owners, engineers, operations teams, and anyone learning how money moves through banks.

Who runs it

Payments Training Hub is run by Debarghya Mondal, a Senior Business Analyst at Capco in Bangalore, deployed to NatWest UK since December 2021. More on the Founder page.

What it teaches

The academy covers payments end to end: what money is, how accounts and ledgers work, the payment lifecycle, ISO 20022 and SWIFT messaging, domestic schemes in India, Europe, the UK and the US, cross-border payments and correspondent banking, clearing and settlement, cards, APIs and open banking, risk and compliance, operations, exceptions and reconciliation, and payments product and business analysis.

The curriculum is 15 domains, 95 chapters and 706 narrated lessons. Every lesson has narrated audio and an animated diagram. Around the lessons sit 10 simulators plus 1 separate ISO 20022 lab, 10 hands-on labs, 14 ISO 20022 exercises, 6 capstone projects, 124 scenarios, 320 interview-ready questions, a 15-test timed series and a placement test.

Who it is for

Banking and fintech professionals who need to understand payments properly, not just pass a test. The material is written for working professionals: it names the messages, the systems and the failure modes, and it works through real operational scenarios.

Independence

Payments Training Hub is an independent education resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any bank, payment scheme, card network, vendor or regulator. Scheme and system names appear because they are the subject matter, not because of any relationship.

A note on accuracy

Payments rules change. Lessons are written from the scheme documentation listed on the Sources page and reviewed against it, but scheme rulebooks are the authority. Check the current rulebook before relying on anything here for a live payment. If you find an error, please report it through the Contact page.
