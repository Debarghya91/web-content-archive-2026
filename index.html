Payments Training Hub is a professional payments training platform for banking and fintech professionals. Learn payments the way banks actually do them: design a payment processing system, follow a payment at every stage, read ISO 20022 and SWIFT messages field by field, understand how banking systems interact, find out why payments fail, write integration requirements, understand clearing and settlement, and handle real banking transformation projects with confidence.

Built by Debarghya Mondal, a Senior Business Analyst who has run payments transformation at NatWest in the UK since 2021.

What Payments Training Hub is

Payments Training Hub is a complete payments academy and payments training course, built from real banking work. Every lesson is a short narrated lesson with an animated diagram that moves as the narration plays, so you see the payment move, the field highlighted, the ledger updated, instead of reading a wall of text.

What you will be able to do. Follow a payment from initiation to settlement across every rail; read and write ISO 20022 and SWIFT messages field by field; map MT to ISO 20022; design payment APIs and integration requirements; investigate exceptions, returns and recalls; and run reconciliation and operational controls like a payments business analyst.

How it is organised. 15 domains, 100 chapters, 881 narrated lessons, 320 interview-ready questions, 10 simulators plus 1 separate ISO 20022 lab, 10 labs, 14 ISO exercises, 6 capstones and 124 reusable payment scenarios. Start at the beginning, or take the placement test and we will tell you exactly which course to start with.

Your first 15 minutes

New here? Fifteen minutes is enough to see what the academy feels like. Four steps, in order.

  1. 2 minutes: watch one payment end to end. Open Process Flows and watch the end-to-end corporate payment flow. You will see the instruction, the messages, the clearing and the settlement in one diagram.
  2. 5 minutes: read Lesson 1. What is a Payment explains the basic building block of every payment system, with narration and an animated diagram.
  3. 5 minutes: run a payment yourself. Payment Lifecycle Simulator: one payment, USD 25,000, London to Singapore, stage by stage.
  4. 3 minutes: try a practice mission. Mission Control puts you on the operations desk for one payment, with a score at the end.

Have more time? Take the placement test and we will tell you exactly which course to start with.
