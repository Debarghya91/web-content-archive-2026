Founder profile, methodology, curriculum references and learner feedback.

Payments Training Hub is a single-author academy. One working payments practitioner writes every lesson, builds every simulator, and answers corrections. The author is a Senior Business Analyst in payments transformation with 8.5 years in banking and payments, delivering ISO 20022 migrations, SWIFT cross-border transformations and core clearing integrations.

What the author brings: ISO 20022 and SWIFT messaging, clearing and settlement, cards and payment architecture, APIs and integration, and payments business analysis. Every lesson is cross-checked against official SWIFT, ISO 20022 and scheme rulebooks, so you learn operational reality, not outdated textbook definitions.

Why this academy exists: payments are taught badly in most places. The material is either too shallow to use on a real project, or it is a rulebook that nobody can read. This academy teaches payments the way banks actually run them: the messages, the systems, the failure modes, and the operational consequences, worked through with real examples.

About Payments Training Hub

Payments Training Hub is a practical payments academy for banking professionals, business analysts, product owners and fintech specialists. The curriculum covers the full payments world in one place: ISO 20022 and SWIFT messaging, SEPA and instant payments, RTGS, NEFT, NACH, IMPS and UPI, clearing and settlement, cards, reconciliation, investigations and the BA craft that ties it together.

Every lesson is narrated and written by a working payments business analyst with 8.5 years in banking and payments, checked against scheme documentation. The course holds 100 chapters and 881 lessons, 564 real-world payment situations, 105 timed tests and 320 interview questions, with simulators and labs for hands-on practice. Courses start at Rs 499, the full Integrated Course costs Rs 2,999, and every product includes one year of access.
