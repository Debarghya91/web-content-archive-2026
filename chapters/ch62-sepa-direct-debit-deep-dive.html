SEPA Direct Debit Deep Dive

D05 Domestic payment systems · 7 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 2026-11-05

Learner explains the SDD pull model, the Core and B2B schemes, and the mandate lifecycle from e-mandate signing to collection. Learner traces the EUR 89.99 Stadtwerke Linden collection through pain.008 and pacs.003, and handles rejects, returns, refunds and reversals with the correct reason codes and deadlines, including the 8 week refund rule.

SEPA direct debit deep dive: mandates, collections, returns and refunds, and the rights that come with them.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains SDD Core and B2B, describes mandate flows and the e-mandate service, and traces a EUR 89.99 monthly collection from pain.008 through pacs.003 to settlement, including rejects, returns, refunds and reversals.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where Stadtwerke Linden must collect EUR 89.99 monthly from Jonas Weber, then drafts the pain.008 file, the pacs.003 message, and the pacs.002 and pacs.004 reports, including a refund inside eight weeks and a reversal with reason code AM05, with realistic mandate references and due dates. Learner also explains the Core and B2B differences and the e-mandate messages in a short note.

Expected deliverables.

  • pain.008 draft for the EUR 89.99 monthly utility collection
  • pacs.003 step by step guide for Core and B2B collections
  • pacs.002 reject and pacs.004 return drafts with reason codes AM05, MS02 and MS03
  • Refund case note: eight week no questions asked and thirteen month unauthorised rules
  • Reversal draft using pain.007 and pacs.007 for a duplicate collection

Completion criterion. Learner drafts a correct pain.008 and pacs.003 for the EUR 89.99 collection, explains each reject and return reason, and handles the eight week refund and the AM05 reversal correctly.
