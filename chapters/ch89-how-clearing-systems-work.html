How clearing systems work

D07 Clearing, settlement and liquidity · 6 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 8 November 2026

Learner explains how clearing systems work, distinguishes netting from gross settlement, computes multilateral net positions, explains settlement cycles, cut-offs, settlement accounts, intraday liquidity, queueing and gridlock resolution, and describes default handling and the RTGS versus deferred net settlement trade-off, with correct terminology.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains how clearing systems work, distinguishes netting from gross settlement, computes multilateral net positions, explains settlement cycles, cut-offs, settlement accounts, intraday liquidity, queueing and gridlock resolution, and describes default handling and the RTGS versus deferred net settlement trade-off, with correct terminology.

Practical assessment. Learner receives ten short scenarios, including a four-bank netting exercise, a missed cut-off that moves the value date, a queued payment resolved by offsetting, and a participant default with loss sharing, and works out the settlement result for each. Learner then writes a one-page note comparing RTGS and deferred net settlement. Learner submits all work with sources, calculations, and assumptions stated clearly, and reviews the result against the chapter checklist before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • Worked multilateral netting calculation for four banks
  • Settlement-day timeline showing funding, cycles and cut-offs
  • Gridlock resolution example with offsetting payments

Completion criterion. Learner correctly computes net positions and explains the settlement outcome for at least nine of ten scenarios, using correct terms and a concrete worked example. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear written explanation throughout.

In this chapter
  1. Netting versus gross settlement: bilateral and multilateral netting with worked numbers
  2. Settlement cycles and cut-off times: how a missed cut-off moves the value date
  3. Settlement accounts and central bank money: how a bank funds its settlement account
  4. Liquidity management in clearing: intraday liquidity, queues and gridlock resolution
  5. What happens when a participant fails: default handling and loss sharing
  6. RTGS versus deferred net settlement: when each is used and the trade-offs
