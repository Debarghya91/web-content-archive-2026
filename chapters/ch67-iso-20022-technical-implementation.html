ISO 20022 Technical Implementation

D04 Messaging and standards · 8 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 2026-11-05

Learner reads an ISO 20022 XSD, explains currency and amount precision, UTC offsets and timestamps, and the market practice layers of PMPG, CBPR+ and SEPA. Learner traces the EUR 10,000 Atelier Lumiere payment as a pacs.008 under both CBPR+ and SEPA, explains LEI and FATF Recommendation 16, and validates an ISO 20022 XML file against schema and business rules.

ISO 20022 technical implementation: how the standard is built, read and validated in real systems.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner reads an ISO 20022 schema, explains amounts, timestamps and market practice, and shows how the same pacs.008 differs by rail, with correct ledger entries and validation handling. Learner also explains wash accounts, LEI, and FATF Recommendation 16 debtor and creditor data.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where a EUR 10,000 payment must be expressed as pacs.008.001.08 under CBPR+ rules and under SEPA rules, then drafts both messages, lists every field that changes, and explains why each change is required. Learner also drafts the T-account entries, the LEI and travel rule data, and the handling when a message fails schema validation. Learner also notes the current state in 2026, with SWIFT MT coexistence ended and Fedwire live on ISO 20022.

Expected deliverables.

  • pacs.008 drafts for EUR 10,000 under CBPR+ and SEPA rules
  • Field difference table: CBPR+ versus SEPA with reasons
  • T-account entries and wash account note for the EUR 10,000 payment
  • Schema validation and cardinality checklist with failure handling
  • Per rail mapping table: CBPR+, SEPA, Fedwire, CHAPS and FedNow

Completion criterion. Learner drafts correct CBPR+ and SEPA pacs.008 messages, lists the field differences accurately, and explains validation, cardinality and ledger entries clearly, and the per rail mapping table is complete and accurate.
