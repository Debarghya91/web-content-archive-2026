1 admi messages: the administration layer of ISO 20022By the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain admi messages: the administration layer of ISO 20022. Administration messages, the admi set in the ISO 20022 catalogue, carry no payment value at all, and that is exactly why they matter: they keep the messaging system itself alive, acknowledged and recoverable. Takeaway: admi is the control that makes every other message trustworthy, so for every message name the acknowledgement, the timeout, the resend rule and the escalation. 6:53Completed ›

The camt.057 Notification to Receive is the message a bank sends to its own customer, or to another bank, to say that a credit is expected, before the money actually arrives. Takeaway: camt.057 lets a treasurer plan around money that has not arrived yet, and it must be corrected when the expectation changes. 7:51Completed ›
3 pacs.028 Payment Status Request: asking where a payment isBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain pacs.028 payment status request: asking where a payment is. Takeaway: pacs.028 is the first message to send when a payment goes quiet, before the phone call, because the structured answer is what the case file needs. 6:29Completed ›

The camt.027 Claim Non-Receipt is the ISO 20022 message a creditor bank sends when the money it expected has not arrived, and it is the formal claim that starts a non-receipt investigation. Takeaway: a camt.027 claim turns a missing payment into a case with a deadline, and the expected value date is what makes it a claim. 6:16Completed ›

Takeaway: camt.087 fixes a payment by agreement with the bank that holds it, and a rejected modification needs a different tool, usually a recall. 6:30Completed ›

A payment case is the operational container that holds an investigation from the moment it is opened until it is closed, and the case lifecycle is the discipline of moving it through defined states with defined owners. Takeaway: a case must be owned at opening, worked in priority order, and closed only when the money is found, returned or recalled, with every action in the file. 6:47Completed ›

Takeaway: gpi Instant makes the last mile instant, and the UETR links the gpi leg and the instant leg in one tracker view. 6:32Completed ›
