Claims, enquiries and case management: how a bank finds a missing payment, answers an enquiry, and runs a case to closure.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains the admi administration layer and uses camt.057, pacs.028, camt.027 and camt.087 to find a missing payment, answer an enquiry and fix a payment after it was sent. Learner runs a case from opening to closure, including stop and recall, and explains gpi Instant, SWIFT Go, Payment Pre-validation and Transaction Manager.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where a EUR 400 SWIFT Go payment has not arrived, then opens a case, sends a pacs.028 status request, raises a camt.027 claim, and explains the camt.029 response and the closure steps. Learner also explains when to use Payment Pre-validation and what Transaction Manager shows.

Expected deliverables.

  • Case lifecycle diagram
  • Message map: admi, camt.057, pacs.028, camt.027, camt.087
  • Investigation case note for EUR 400
  • Comparison note: gpi Instant, SWIFT Go and Pre-validation

Completion criterion. Learner runs the case correctly through pacs.028, camt.027 and camt.029, explains each message, and states when gpi Instant, SWIFT Go and Pre-validation apply.

In this chapter
  1. admi messages: the administration layer of ISO 20022
  2. camt.057 Notification to Receive: knowing the money is coming
  3. pacs.028 Payment Status Request: asking where a payment is
  4. camt.027 Claim Non-Receipt: when the money never arrived
  5. camt.087 Request to Modify Payment: fixing a payment after it was sent
  6. Case lifecycle and stop or recall: running an investigation to closure
  7. gpi Instant: cross-border payments on instant rails
  8. SWIFT Go: low-value cross-border payments, fast and predictable
  9. Payment Pre-validation: checking the beneficiary before sending
  10. Transaction Manager: the shared record of an ISO 20022 payment
