SEPA Credit Transfer Deep Dive

D05 Domestic payment systems · 7 lessons

Version 1.1 | Last reviewed 2026-11-05

Learner explains the four-corner SCT model and traces the EUR 48,250 Loire Components payment from pain.001 through pacs.008 to camt reporting, naming each message, status and exchange period. Learner describes exceptions and investigations with camt.056, camt.029 and pacs.004, and explains the SCT Inst mandate and the 2024 transition to the 2019 message versions.

SEPA credit transfer deep dive: message flows, exchange periods and the 2024 transition, under the SCT rulebook.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains the SCT four-corner model, reads ISO 20022 message names, and traces a EUR 48,250 payment from pain.001 through pacs.008 to camt.053, including exchange periods, cut-off times, and the 2019 message transition.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where Loire Components must pay EUR 48,250 to Rhine Metals, then drafts the pain.001 file, the pacs.008 message, and the pain.002 and pacs.002 reports, including a camt.056 cancellation and a camt.029 answer, with realistic references, value dates and cut-off times. Learner also explains the SCT Inst mandate, the SEPA area, and the 2019 message changes in a short note.

Expected deliverables.

  • pain.001 draft for the EUR 48,250 payment to Rhine Metals
  • pacs.008 step by step guide for SEPA pacs.008.001.08
  • pain.002 and pacs.002 status reports for acceptance and settlement
  • camt.056 cancellation request and camt.029 answer for the duplicate payment
  • Note on SCT Inst mandate, SEPA schemes, and EEA versus SEPA area versus eurozone

Completion criterion. Learner drafts a correct pain.001 and pacs.008 for the EUR 48,250 payment, explains each status report, and handles the camt.056 cancellation and the SCT Inst rules accurately.
