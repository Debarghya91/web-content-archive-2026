Payment platform architecture and your career: designing platforms, earning the premium, and planning your next steps in payments.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains payment platform architecture choices and maps them to career paths, including business analyst, product manager, architect, and operations roles in payments. Learner demonstrates this skill using realistic banking examples, explaining each step clearly and using correct terminology throughout.

Practical assessment. Learner compares three platform architectures, monolith, modular, and microservices, for a bank processing 500,000 payments per day, and recommends one with reasons covering scale, change, and risk. Learner then writes a career plan naming a target role, three skills to build, and two portfolio artefacts, using the platform knowledge from this chapter. Learner submits the work with all calculations, sources, and assumptions stated clearly, and checks the result against the chapter checklist before final submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • Platform architecture comparison table
  • Architecture recommendation with reasons
  • Career plan with target role and skills
  • Portfolio artefact list with two items

Completion criterion. Learner compares all three architectures accurately, justifies the recommendation with scale and risk, and writes a specific career plan with named skills. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear reasoning throughout the submitted work.
