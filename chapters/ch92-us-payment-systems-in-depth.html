US payment systems in depth

D05 Domestic payment systems · 7 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 8 November 2026

Learner explains the US payment systems in depth, distinguishes Fedwire, CHIPS, ACH, Same Day ACH, FedNow and RTP, applies NACHA return and notification rules, and routes payments across the US rails to realistic business scenarios with correct terminology.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains the US payment systems in depth, distinguishes Fedwire, CHIPS, ACH, Same Day ACH, FedNow and RTP, applies NACHA return and notification rules, and routes payments across the US rails to realistic business scenarios with correct terminology.

Practical assessment. Learner receives ten short scenarios, including a 45 million dollar Fedwire payment, a 6.2 million dollar payroll file, an unauthorised ACH return, a 4,500 dollar Sunday-night FedNow payment and a 750,000 dollar RTP routing decision, and selects the rail and the outcome for each. Learner then writes a one-page note comparing FedNow and RTP. Learner submits all work with sources, calculations, and assumptions stated clearly, and reviews the result against the chapter checklist before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • US rail comparison table for Fedwire, CHIPS, ACH, FedNow and RTP
  • Same Day ACH window and limit summary
  • Payment routing decision for a treasurer moving funds across four systems

Completion criterion. Learner correctly selects the right US rail and explains the timing, limits and costs for at least nine of ten scenarios, using correct terms and a concrete example. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear written explanation throughout.

In this chapter
  1. Fedwire in depth: real-time gross settlement, the Federal Reserve and daylight overdraft
  2. CHIPS in depth: private-sector large-value clearing, netting and final settlement
  3. ACH in depth: the NACHA rules, SEC codes, batches and files, returns and notifications of change
  4. Same Day ACH: the windows, the limits and what it changed for businesses
  5. FedNow in depth: the Federal Reserve instant payment service, participation and liquidity
  6. RTP from The Clearing House: how it differs from FedNow and when a bank uses both
  7. The US faster payments landscape: how Fedwire, CHIPS, ACH, FedNow and RTP fit together
