The capstone project: you are the BA on a bank migration from SWIFT MT to ISO 20022, scoping it, mapping it, testing it and taking it live.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner completes a capstone project that designs an end-to-end payment solution for a stated business problem, integrating messages, rails, controls, and operations into one coherent proposal. Learner demonstrates this skill using realistic banking examples, explaining each step clearly and using correct terminology throughout.

Practical assessment. Learner designs a payment solution for a company paying 2,000 suppliers across Europe and Asia each month, covering rails, message formats, charges, screening, exceptions, and reconciliation. Learner presents the solution in a written proposal with a flow diagram, a cost estimate for a 50,000 euro monthly volume, and a rollout plan with three phases and risks. Learner submits the work with all calculations, sources, and assumptions stated clearly, and checks the result against the chapter checklist before final submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • Capstone proposal document
  • End-to-end payment flow diagram
  • Cost estimate for monthly volume
  • Rollout plan with phases and risks

Completion criterion. Learner covers rails, messages, controls, and reconciliation coherently, justifies the rail choices, and presents a phased rollout with realistic risks. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear reasoning throughout the submitted work.
