Correspondent banking and nostro vostro accounts: how banks pay each other across borders when they have no branch in the other country.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains correspondent banking, including nostro and vostro accounts, account funding, and the chain of banks in a cross-border payment, and calculates the funding impact of a payment. Learner applies this skill in realistic banking scenarios, with clear examples and correct terminology throughout.

Practical assessment. Learner traces a 100,000 dollar payment from a bank in India to a beneficiary in Brazil through two correspondent banks, showing the nostro and vostro entries at each bank. Learner then calculates the nostro balance change after three payments totalling 250,000 dollars and explains what happens when the nostro balance is insufficient. Learner submits all work with sources, calculations, and assumptions stated clearly, and reviews the result against the chapter checklist before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • Correspondent chain diagram for the India to Brazil payment
  • Nostro and vostro accounting entries
  • Nostro balance calculation worked example
  • Insufficient funds handling note

Completion criterion. Learner traces the chain correctly, posts the nostro and vostro entries accurately, calculates the balance change, and explains the shortfall consequence. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear written explanation throughout.
