Lifecycle timelines and failure modes: minute-by-minute timelines for one instant, one batch and one cross-border payment.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner builds minute-by-minute timelines for an instant, a batch and a cross-border payment, and compares four rails carrying the same EUR 10,000 payment from Paris to Berlin. Learner explains the seven lifecycle stages and states the failure, the message and the recovery at each stage.

Practical assessment. Learner receives the EUR 4,500 SCT Inst payment, the EUR 1.2 million salary file of 500 payments, and the USD 85,000 cross-border payment, then draws the timeline for each, names the messages and statuses at each step, and explains what happens when each one fails at a different stage.

Expected deliverables.

  • Three timelines: instant, batch and cross-border
  • Rail comparison table for EUR 10,000
  • Failure matrix by lifecycle stage
  • Note on where the time goes in a cross-border payment

Completion criterion. Learner draws correct timelines for all three payments, names the messages and statuses at each step, and explains the failure and recovery at each lifecycle stage.
