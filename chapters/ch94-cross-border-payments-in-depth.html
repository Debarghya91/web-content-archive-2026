Why take this chapter

Correspondent banking, SWIFT gpi, CBPR+, cover payments and nostro liquidity. This chapter has 10 lessons, from Correspondent banking in depth: how the network really works to SADC-RTGS and COMESA: regional settlement in Southern and Eastern Africa.

Learning objective. Learner explains cross-border payments in depth, traces correspondent banking chains, applies SWIFT gpi tracking and UETR, explains CBPR+ and the MT to MX migration, distinguishes serial from cover payments, prices FX, optimises nostro liquidity and applies sanctions screening across jurisdictions with correct terminology.

Practical assessment. Learner receives ten short scenarios, including a 200 dollar remittance losing fees along the chain, a 10,000 pound payment with an FX spread, a gpi-tracked payment with a UETR, a truncated CBPR+ translation and a nostro balance costing funding drag, and works out the route, cost and outcome for each. Learner then writes a one-page note on serial versus cover payments. Learner submits all work with sources, calculations, and assumptions stated clearly, and reviews the result against the chapter checklist before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • Correspondent banking chain diagram for one cross-border payment
  • Serial versus cover payment comparison with MT103 and MT202 COV
  • Nostro optimisation worked example with pooling and sweeping

Completion criterion. Learner correctly traces and prices at least nine of ten cross-border payments and explains the routing, FX and compliance outcome, using correct terms and a concrete worked example. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear written explanation throughout.
