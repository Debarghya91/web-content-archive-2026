Trade Finance Deep Dive

D06 Cross-border payments · 7 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 2026-11-05

Learner explains why exporters and importers need banks, how a documentary credit works, and the roles of the issuing, confirming, notifying and advising banks. Learner describes documentary collections, simple collections, bills of exchange and promissory notes, and traces the EUR 180,000 machinery export from order to payment.

Trade finance deep dive: how banks help exporters and importers trade safely across borders.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains trade finance, describes documentary credits with confirming and notifying banks, documentary and simple collections, and shows how bills of exchange and promissory notes work in practice. Learner also describes negotiable instruments and cash management basics for trade customers.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where a German exporter sells EUR 180,000 of machinery to Poland under a confirmed documentary credit and EUR 12,000 of spare parts under a simple collection, then drafts the MT700 credit, the confirmation steps, the MT400 collection instruction, and the bill of exchange for the spare parts. Learner also explains the cash management view across both deals.

Expected deliverables.

  • Documentary credit flow: MT700, confirmation and document check
  • Simple collection flow: MT400, acceptance and payment at maturity
  • Bill of exchange and promissory note comparison with four-corner models
  • Negotiable instruments note: endorsement and holder in due course
  • Cash management view for the EUR 180,000 and EUR 12,000 deals

Completion criterion. Learner drafts a correct confirmed credit flow and a correct simple collection, explains the bill of exchange four-corner model, and shows the cash timing for both deals.

  6. Bills of Exchange and Promissory Notes: Parties and the Four-Corner Model
  7. Worked Example: A EUR 180,000 Machinery Export and a EUR 12,000 Spare Parts Collection
