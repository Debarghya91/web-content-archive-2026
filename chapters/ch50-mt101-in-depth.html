MT101 in depth: how corporations instruct banks to pay, field by field.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains MT101 use cases, multiple debit accounts, subsidiary payments, funds repatriation and the difference between Sequence A and Sequence B. Learner uses real examples with amounts, banks and message names to show how the topic works in practice.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where a UK parent must pay 20 suppliers from three subsidiary accounts in euros, pounds and dollars, then drafts an MT101 with Sequence A and Sequence B, maps each field to a pain.001 equivalent, and explains how the bank executes and reports the payments. Learner submits all work with sources, calculations and assumptions stated clearly, and reviews the result against the chapter checklist before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • MT101 draft for a multi-account payment run
  • Field mapping table: MT101 to pain.001
  • Diagram of parent, subsidiaries and paying bank
  • Error handling note for rejected instructions

Completion criterion. Learner produces a correct MT101 draft with both sequences mapped to pain.001, and explains execution, reporting and error handling clearly. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness and clear written explanation throughout.
