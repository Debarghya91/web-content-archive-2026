3 FedNow: instant payments around the clockBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain FEDNOW: instant payments around the clock. FedNow Service is the Federal Reserve's instant payment system, launched in July 2023. The analyst must design the funding and alerting rules with treasury, because a bank that runs out of FedNow liquidity on a Sunday night cannot wait until Monday morning. 8:26Completed ›
4 ACH: the batch network that pays AmericaBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain ACH: the batch network that pays america. The Automated Clearing House, or ACH, is the electronic network that moves most of the routine payments in the United States. The whole cycle takes one to two business days for a standard ACH payment, and the analyst must make this timing clear, because the customer experience can look fast while settlement is still a day away. 4:38Completed ›
5 CHIPS: private sector netting for large paymentsBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain CHIPS: private sector netting for large payments. Clearing House Interbank Payments System, or CHIPS, is the private sector system operated by The Clearing House for large value US dollar payments. The analyst must show the cutoff in the product and in the operations manual in Eastern Time, and must design the queue view so operations can see which payments are waiting and what funding is needed to release them. 4:15Completed ›

A good analyst would route these three payments through three different systems. 2:08Completed ›

9 Lynx, Canada's high-value RTGS systemBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain lynx, canada's high-value RTGS system. Lynx is the system that settles large and time critical payments in Canadian dollars between financial institutions in Canada. The cost is liquidity: a bank must fund each payment in full, not only a smaller net amount at the end of the day. 5:03Completed ›
10 NSS, the National Settlement ServiceBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain nss, the national settlement service. The National Settlement Service settles the money behind private sector clearing arrangements in the United States, using accounts at the Federal Reserve. Timing is what most often confuses analysts, because clearing, customer posting and interbank settlement happen at different moments. 4:29Completed ›
11 Phixius, the ACH information exchangeBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain phixius, the ACH information exchange. Phixius is a payment information network run by Nacha, the organisation that governs the ACH network in the United States. Coverage is therefore the limit an analyst should check first: where no responder covers an account, the bank must fall back on other validation methods, such as a prenote or a small test transaction. 4:20Completed ›

Every electronic payment in the United States needs to identify the receiving bank, and the key for that is the ABA routing number: nine digits that identify a specific financial institution. The two numbers are always used together, and the analyst must treat them as a pair. 4:28Completed ›
