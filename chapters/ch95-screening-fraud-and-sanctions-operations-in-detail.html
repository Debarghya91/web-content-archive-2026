Screening, fraud and sanctions operations in detail

D12 Risk, compliance and regulation · 8 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 8 November 2026

Learner explains how AML screening, sanctions hits, fraud detection, fraud case handling, APP scams, mule accounts, transaction monitoring and control operations are actually handled step by step inside a bank, and applies the workflows to realistic alerts and cases with correct terminology.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains how AML screening, sanctions hits, fraud detection, fraud case handling, APP scams, mule accounts, transaction monitoring and control operations are actually handled step by step inside a bank, and applies the workflows to realistic alerts and cases with correct terminology.

Practical assessment. Learner receives ten short scenarios, including a sanctions true match, a fuzzy-match false positive, a fraud rules-engine alert, a mule account network, a monitoring threshold breach and a failed maker-checker control, and works out the handling steps for each. Learner then writes a one-page note on how a sanctions hit is dispositioned. Learner submits all work with sources, calculations, and assumptions stated clearly, and reviews the result against the chapter checklist before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • Sanctions hit workflow from alert to resolution
  • Fraud case handling timeline from alert to reimbursement
  • Transaction monitoring scenario and threshold tuning note

Completion criterion. Learner correctly works through at least nine of ten operational scenarios and names the next step, the owner and the evidence required, using correct terms and a concrete example. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear written explanation throughout.

In this chapter
  1. AML screening in detail: name screening, transaction screening and false positives
  2. Sanctions screening in detail: the four lists, the 50 percent rule and field-by-field checks
  3. When a sanctions hit fires: the step-by-step workflow from alert to resolution
  4. Fraud detection in detail: rules engines, behaviour, velocity, device and IP signals
  5. Fraud case handling step by step: alert, customer contact, blocking, reimbursement and loss accounting
  6. APP scams and mule accounts: how mule networks are detected and dismantled
  7. Transaction monitoring in detail: scenarios, thresholds, tuning and disposition
  8. Controls operations in detail: preventive vs detective, maker-checker, testing and failure
