UK payment systems in depth

D05 Domestic payment systems · 7 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 8 November 2026

Learner explains the UK payment systems in depth, distinguishes BACS, CHAPS, Faster Payments and Open Banking payments, applies Confirmation of Payee outcomes, explains the Bank of England RTGS renewal, and applies the APP scam reimbursement rules to realistic UK payment scenarios with correct terminology.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains the UK payment systems in depth, distinguishes BACS, CHAPS, Faster Payments and Open Banking payments, applies Confirmation of Payee outcomes, explains the Bank of England RTGS renewal, and applies the APP scam reimbursement rules to realistic UK payment scenarios with correct terminology.

Practical assessment. Learner receives ten short scenarios, including a 500,000 pound house purchase, a 2,000 pound salary payment, a Faster Payment that hits a bank limit, a Confirmation of Payee no-match and a 60,000 pound APP scam, and selects the rail and the outcome for each. Learner then writes a one-page note on the RTGS renewal. Learner submits all work with sources, calculations, and assumptions stated clearly, and reviews the result against the chapter checklist before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • UK rail comparison table for BACS, CHAPS, Faster Payments and Open Banking
  • Confirmation of Payee outcome decision tree
  • APP scam reimbursement worked example

Completion criterion. Learner correctly selects the right UK rail and explains the timing, limits and controls for at least nine of ten scenarios, using correct terms and a concrete example. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear written explanation throughout.

In this chapter
  1. BACS in depth: Direct Debit, Direct Credit and the three-day cycle
  2. CHAPS in depth: high-value same-day payments and the RTGS
  3. Faster Payments in depth: the 15-second service, limits and access models
  4. Confirmation of Payee: how name checking works and what a no-match means
  5. UK Open Banking payments: payment initiation, consent and the regulatory framework
  6. The Bank of England RTGS renewal programme: what changed and what it means for banks
  7. UK payment fraud: authorised push payment scams and the reimbursement rules
