Cheques and Paper Instruments

D01 Foundations of money and payments · 7 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 2026-11-05

Learner explains what a cheque is, the drawer, drawee and payee roles, bearer and order cheques, and the four-corner cheque model. Learner describes physical and image-based clearing timelines, why a cheque bounces, the Canada modernisation example, and on-us transactions and the push versus pull distinction.

Cheques and paper instruments: paper instructions, the cheque clearing process and the pull model.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains what a cheque is, the main cheque types, the four-corner model, image-based clearing and bounce handling, and describes payment systems modernisation with the Canada example and the GBP 4,750 worked example.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where a UK small business pays a supplier GBP 4,750 by crossed account payee cheque, then traces the four-corner flow, the image-based clearing timeline over two working days, and the bounce path with fees and legal recourse. Learner also compares on-us and cross-bank payments, explains push versus pull, and summarises the Canada modernisation in a short note.

Expected deliverables.

  • Four-corner cheque flow diagram for the GBP 4,750 payment
  • Cheque types comparison table: bearer, order, crossed, certified and bankers cheque
  • Image-based clearing timeline: two working days from deposit to credit
  • Bounce handling note: return codes, fees and legal recourse
  • Canada modernisation summary: Real-Time Rail and ISO 20022 batch rebuild

Completion criterion. Learner traces the GBP 4,750 cheque through the four-corner model and image-based clearing, explains bounce handling and on-us versus cross-bank settlement, and describes the Canada modernisation accurately.
