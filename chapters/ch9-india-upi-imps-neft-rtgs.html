How money moves inside India: UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS, from a ten rupee UPI payment to a crore rupee RTGS transfer.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner compares UPI, IMPS, NEFT, and RTGS in India, explaining limits, settlement timing, operating hours, and use cases, and selects the right rail for a given payment need. Learner practises this skill on realistic banking cases, explaining the reasoning clearly and using correct terminology throughout the work.

Practical assessment. Learner receives six Indian payment needs, including a 200,000 rupee same-day supplier payment, a 500 rupee instant peer transfer, and a 5,000,000 rupee high-value settlement, and recommends UPI, IMPS, NEFT, or RTGS for each with reasons. Learner then traces a failed UPI transaction, identifying the status, the reversal rule, and the customer communication required. Learner presents the work with calculations and assumptions stated clearly, then reviews it against the chapter checklist and corrects any gaps before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS comparison table
  • Rail recommendation sheet for six needs
  • Failed UPI transaction trace and reversal note
  • Cut-off and limit reference sheet

Completion criterion. Learner recommends the correct rail for at least five of six needs, states limits and timing accurately, and explains the failed UPI reversal rule correctly. Judgement covers accuracy, completeness, and clear written explanation in the submitted work.

In this chapter
  1. The Indian payments landscape at a glance
  2. UPI architecture: VPA, PSP banks and NPCI
  3. IMPS: instant transfers around the clock
  4. NEFT: batch settlement and why it still matters
  5. RTGS: high value settlement with finality
  6. NACH: bulk debits, credits and recurring payments
  7. How the rails differ: limits, timing and cost
  8. Choosing the right rail: a worked example
