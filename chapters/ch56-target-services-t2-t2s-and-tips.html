TARGET Services: T2, T2S and TIPS

D05 Domestic payment systems · 7 lessons

Version 1.0 | Last reviewed 2026-11-05

Learner explains TARGET Services, distinguishes T2, T2S and TIPS, and describes the MCA, ESMIG access, and multi currency operation after TARGET2 closed on 20 March 2023. Learner uses real amounts, banks and message names to show how the services work in practice.

TARGET services T2, T2S and TIPS: Europe's new payment infrastructure after TARGET2.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains TARGET Services, distinguishes T2, T2S and TIPS, and describes the MCA, ESMIG access, and multi currency operation after TARGET2 closed on 20 March 2023. Learner uses real amounts, banks and message names to show how the services work in practice.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where a bank in Frankfurt must move EUR 40,000,000 of liquidity between its RTGS DCA and its MCA, open a TIPS DCA, and explain how T2S settles a EUR 5,000,000 securities trade, then drafts the account map, the liquidity transfer steps, and a one page note on when each TARGET service is used. Learner also explains the ESMIG access route and the multi currency scope in the same note.

Expected deliverables.

  • TARGET account map: MCA, RTGS DCA, TIPS DCA and CLM
  • Liquidity transfer worked example for EUR 40,000,000
  • One page note: when to use T2, T2S and TIPS
  • Comparison table: RTGS, CLM, T2S and TIPS

Completion criterion. Learner produces a correct TARGET account map with MCA, RTGS DCA and TIPS DCA, explains each service clearly, and drafts accurate liquidity transfer steps. and the liquidity steps are complete and accurate.

In this chapter
  1. TARGET2 Does Not Exist Anymore: What Went Live on 20 March 2023
  2. The Three TARGET Services: T2, T2S and TIPS
  3. Inside T2: CLM Central Liquidity Management and the Main Cash Account (MCA)
  4. Inside T2: RTGS Real-Time Gross Settlement
  5. T2S Securities Settlement and TIPS Instant Settlement
  6. ESMIG, Multi-Currency Support and the Legal Framework (Guideline (EU) of the ECB on TARGET, repealing Guideline ECB/2012/27)
  7. Worked Example: A EUR 10 Million Payment Through T2 and a EUR 5,000 Instant Payment Through TIPS, traced step by step, and why TARGET is one of the largest payment systems in the world
