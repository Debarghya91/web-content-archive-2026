PSD2, PSD3, DORA, sanctions, AML and the rules that bind a payment. This chapter has 9 lessons, from PSD2 vs PSD3 vs the Payment Services Regulation: what changes and when to PFMI principles applied to a faster payment system.

Learning objective. Learner explains the regulatory framework that binds payments in Europe, the UK, the US and India, distinguishes PSD2 from PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation, and applies DORA, the Instant Payments Regulation, sanctions regimes, AML rules, RBI directions and FFIEC guidance to realistic payment scenarios with correct terminology.

Practical assessment. Learner receives ten short scenarios, including a 50,000 euro SEPA instant payment, a sanctions-screened cross-border transfer, a DORA incident report and an RBI-regulated UPI payment, and identifies the regulation that applies and the action the bank must take. Learner then writes a one-page note comparing PSD2, PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation. Learner submits all work with sources, calculations, and assumptions stated clearly, and reviews the result against the chapter checklist before submission.

Expected deliverables.

  • Regulation comparison table for PSD2, PSD3, the Payment Services Regulation, DORA and the Instant Payments Regulation
  • Sanctions regime summary for OFAC, EU and UN lists
  • One-page note on which regulator applies to a cross-border payment

Completion criterion. Learner correctly identifies the applicable regulation for at least nine of ten scenarios and explains the practical obligation it creates for the bank, using correct terms and a concrete example. Completion is judged on accuracy, completeness, and clear written explanation throughout.
