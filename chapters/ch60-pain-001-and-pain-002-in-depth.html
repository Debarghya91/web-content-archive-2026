What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner explains pain.001 and pain.002 in depth, describes the relay scenario, authorised party initiation, and financial institution initiation, and traces a payment to pacs.008. Learner uses real amounts, banks and message names to show how customer initiation works in practice.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where a corporation in Rotterdam must pay EUR 750,000 to a supplier in Vienna through a relay bank, then drafts the pain.001 file, the relay forwarding steps, the pacs.008 creation, and the pain.002 reports at each stage, including a rejection case with reason code AC01. Learner also explains authorised party initiation and financial institution initiation in a short comparison note.

Expected deliverables.

  • pain.001 draft for the EUR 750,000 relay payment
  • Relay flow diagram: customer, relay bank, debtor agent, creditor agent
  • pain.002 reports for acceptance, settlement in process and settlement
  • Rejection case note: AC01 correction and resend steps

Completion criterion. Learner drafts a correct pain.001 and pain.002 set for the EUR 750,000 relay payment, explains each status report, and handles the AC01 rejection correctly. and the comparison note is clear and accurate.
