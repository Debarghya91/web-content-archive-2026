Cross-border investigations: finding a missing MT103, a missing MT202 COV, and every other cross-border failure.

What You Can Do After This Chapter

Learning objective. Learner runs a cross-border investigation for each of the ten failure types, from a missing MT103 and a missing MT202 COV to a duplicate payment, a compliance hold and a charges discrepancy. Learner explains the serial versus cover split, the messages used at each step, and how a recall, a cancellation and a return of funds differ.

Practical assessment. Learner receives a case where a customer payment arrived but the MT202 COV did not, then traces both legs, identifies where the cover stopped, and drafts the investigation messages and the customer update. Learner also handles a charges discrepancy and a duplicate payment case.

Expected deliverables.

  • Investigation playbook for ten failure types
  • Missing MT202 COV case note
  • Charges discrepancy worked example
  • Recall versus cancellation comparison table

Completion criterion. Learner traces the missing cover correctly, identifies the break point, drafts the right investigation messages, and explains the recall, cancellation and return differences.
