1 Central Bank Digital CurrenciesBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain central bank digital currencies. A central bank digital currency, or CBDC, is digital money issued directly by a central bank, as a liability of the central bank itself, in the same way that physical cash is. For a BA, the practical point is that CBDCs are not a distant theory, they are a real product area, and the requirements skills used for any payment product, user journeys, limits, privacy, reconciliation and reporting, apply directly to them. 4:40Completed ›
2 Stablecoins and Crypto PaymentsBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain stablecoins and crypto payments. This matters because the next payment product you work on may use one of these technologies. 4 Cross Border Innovation: mBridge and Project NexusBy the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain cross border innovation: mbridge and project nexus. Cross border payments have been slow and expensive for decades, because they rely on correspondent banking, where a payment passes through several banks, each taking a fee and adding a delay, and where the sender often. For a BA, the lesson from both projects is that cross border innovation is not about one technology winning, it is about solving the same problems in different ways. 4:31Completed ›
