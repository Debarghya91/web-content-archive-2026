Key terms

Liquidity transfer: A movement of funds between accounts held by the same participant to manage liquidity, for example from a main cash account to a dedicated cash account.

Charge bearer: A code that states who pays the charges for a payment. In ISO 20022 the values are DEBT for debtor, CRED for creditor, SHAR for shared and SLEV for service level.

Settlement: The final movement of funds between banks to discharge obligations created by cleared payments. Settlement is usually made in central bank money or through correspondent accounts.

  1. What to do. List the three levels of the message and name the field that carries the amount and the field that carries the end-to-end reference.

Correct result. The amount is carried in the instructed amount element and the reference in the end-to-end identification. The three levels are the group header, the payment information, and the credit transfer transaction information.

Why. A message is only useful if you can read it. The group header identifies the message, the payment information identifies the batch, and the transaction information carries the single payment.

Once Meridian Bank Paris has debited the Loire Components account, it builds the pacs.008.001.08 Customer Credit Transfer message for the interbank leg. The pacs.008 copies the debtor, creditor, amount, currency, and end to end identification from the pain.001, and it adds interbank fields that the customer never sees. Those fields include the debtor agent, Meridian Bank Paris, the creditor agent, Harbour Bank Frankfurt, the settlement method, the clearing system reference, the interbank settlement date, and the charge bearer.

The instructed amount is EUR 48,250, and the interbank settlement amount is the same, because this is a single currency SEPA payment with shared charges. The pacs.008 is sent into the clearing system during the next exchange period, and the clearing system routes it to Harbour Bank Frankfurt. Settlement of the obligation between the two banks happens through central bank money, usually on a net basis across many payments in the same exchange period. Meridian Bank Paris does not send EUR 48,250 directly to Harbour Bank Frankfurt as a single wire.

Instead, the clearing system calculates what each bank owes the others after netting all payments in the period, and the net amounts settle on central bank accounts. The pacs.008 message and the settlement are separate things, and a business analyst must keep them separate in any design. Harbour Bank Frankfurt receives the pacs.008 and runs its own checks before crediting Rhine Metals. It validates the message structure, checks the creditor IBAN against the account it holds, screens the payment for sanctions, and confirms that the account is open and able to receive funds.

If the checks pass, it credits EUR 48,250 to the Rhine Metals account on the value date of Tuesday 10 November 2026, and it records the credit with the end to end identification LOIRE-RHINE-2026-1148. It then sends a pacs.002.001.10 Payment Status Report back to Meridian Bank Paris with status ACCP, confirming that the payment was accepted and the beneficiary account was credited. A pacs.004 Payment Return is the message that sends funds back when the beneficiary bank cannot credit the payment.

Suppose the Rhine Metals account had been closed the week before the payment arrived. Harbour Bank Frankfurt could not credit EUR 48,250, so it would return the funds with a pacs.004.001.09 message, carrying the original end to end identification, the returned amount, and a reason code such as AC04 for a closed account. The pacs.004 is a separate settlement instruction, not a copy of the pacs.008, and it moves the money back through the clearing system to Meridian Bank Paris, which then credits Loire Components and reports the return in a pain.002 message.
