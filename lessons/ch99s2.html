  1. What to do. State what happens at each stage, who pays whom, and when the merchant is actually credited.

Correct result. Authorisation holds the funds and returns a response code. Clearing sends the transaction in a batch file. Settlement moves the net amount between banks, and the acquirer credits the merchant after deducting interchange, scheme fees and its margin.

Why. A card payment is not one event. The customer sees it on Monday, the merchant gets it on Wednesday, and the difference is the clearing and settlement cycle.

Approval limits and authorisation are the two controls that stop a single mistake, or a single bad decision, from becoming a large loss. A limit caps how much can move at once. Authorisation says who may allow it to move, and it must be independent of the thing being authorised. Start with limits. Set a per-transaction limit, a daily limit, and a limit per payee or per channel.

For example, an AI-assisted payment flow might allow payments up to 5,000 pounds to go through with one approval, payments from 5,000 to 25,000 pounds to need a second approver, and any payment above 25,000 pounds to need a senior approver as well. The daily limit might cap the total at 250,000 pounds, so even a run of small payments cannot add up to a large loss unseen.

Then set the authorisation rule. The person or control that approves the payment must not be the same person or system that created it, checked it, or benefits from it. That independence is the point. If the same AI agent that prepared a payment of 40,000 pounds also approves it, there is no check at all, only a second step with the same judgement. Authorisation must also be recorded. Who approved it, when, on what evidence, and under which limit.

If a payment goes wrong, that record is how you find out whether the limit was followed and whether the approver saw the right information. Review limits regularly. A limit set when volumes were low may be far too high when volumes grow, and a limit that is never hit may be hiding the fact that no one is watching. Limits and authorisation only work when they are separate, written down, and checked.
