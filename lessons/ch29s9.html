Key terms

Reconciliation: The process of comparing records from two sources, such as a bank statement and an internal ledger, to confirm they agree.

Payment hub: A central system in a bank that receives payment instructions from all channels, validates them, applies screening and routing rules, and sends them to the correct clearing system or correspondent.

Liquidity: Cash or assets that a bank can use immediately to meet payment obligations. Banks manage liquidity by forecasting inflows and outflows, funding nostro and settlement accounts, and using central bank...

Exercise

Read the ISO 20022 messages message described in this lesson and identify its key parts.

Use the message structure, fields and example in this lesson.

  1. What to do. List the main blocks of the message, name the field that carries the amount, and name the field that identifies the payment.

Correct result. The message is made of blocks, each carrying a part of the payment instruction. The amount field and the identification field are the two a receiving bank checks first, because without them it cannot process or reconcile the payment.

Why. A message is only useful if you can read it. The blocks tell the receiving bank what to do, and the amount and identification fields are what it checks first.

Transcript and full text

Show transcript

The capstone brief places you as the business analyst for a regional bank in Ohio joining the RTP network to offer real-time payments. The bank serves 250,000 retail and 8,000 business customers through ACH batch payments and wire transfers, so a supplier paid on a Friday waits until Monday. The board has approved the programme, the budget covers the hub changes, the core banking integration, the prefunding arrangement and the testing, and go-live is ten months away. Your deliverables are the evidence the bank is ready, and each one is reviewed by the programme board before the next phase starts.

The network decision weighs RTP against FedNow before any build starts, and it shapes the architecture, the liquidity model and the customer promise. RTP is operated by The Clearing House and settles in prefunded positions, while FedNow is operated by the Federal Reserve and settles in central bank money. The bank weighs reach, relationships, liquidity, and features, including whether request for payment is needed from the first release. Many banks join both networks, and this bank joins RTP first and FedNow second, with a routing directory that checks enrolment and logs each decision.

The target architecture connects the channels, the hub, the RTP gateway, the core banking system and the liquidity position. The channel layer is the mobile app, internet banking and business portal, where the customer instructs payments and approves requests for payment. The payment hub validates the instruction, checks the routing directory and holds the status, and the RTP gateway converts the instruction into ISO 20022 messages and manages connectivity. The core banking system holds the ledger, and the liquidity layer is the prefunded position, monitored continuously, with top-up procedures that work at night and weekends.

The message set is the ISO 20022 set the RTP network requires, and each message has one job. The pacs.008 message carries the credit transfer, the pacs.002 message carries the status report, and the pacs.004 message carries the return. The camt.056 message carries the recall request, and the request for payment flow carries the biller request to the payer bank, so the payer approval triggers the credit transfer. The mapping document must show every field, its validation rule, and the status each message triggers, because a message without its status transition leaves payments stuck.

A worked example follows a supplier payment and a request for payment through the new architecture. A manufacturer in Columbus pays a Cleveland supplier USD 96,000 on a Saturday morning, and the hub checks the routing directory and the prefunded position, and releases the payment. The supplier bank credits the supplier within seconds, a status report confirms acceptance, and the core banking system posts the debit. The same afternoon, a property manager sends a request for payment of USD 1,400 to a tenant, the tenant approves it in the app, and the payment travels as an RTP credit transfer.

Certification and the test plan prove the bank is ready, and neither can be shortened without hiding risk. Certification covers connectivity, message conformance, screening in milliseconds, liquidity and prefunding readiness, and operational readiness for a service that never closes. The test plan covers the happy path, returns and rejects, timeouts, duplicates, request for payment decisions, recalls, and reconciliation to the prefunded position. Each test case names the precondition, the steps, the expected status and the expected ledger entry, and a test passes only when the status, the ledger, the position and the customer message all match.

The stakeholders, timeline and risks decide whether the plan survives contact with reality. The sponsor is the head of commercial banking, the operations team of 25 staff will run the continuous service, and the treasury team owns the prefunded position. The timeline runs for ten months, from analysis and mapping through build, testing, certification and rehearsal to go-live, with the mapping sign-off and the certification pass as hard milestones. The largest risks are a low prefunded position, a receiver directory that misses an unenrolled bank, and a slow screening engine, and each risk needs an owner, a mitigation and a date.

The deliverable set is the evidence the BA did the job, and it must be consistent from the first document to the last. The deliverables are the business requirements document, the message mapping document, the flow diagrams, the user stories, the test cases, the training material, the prefunding procedure, and the go-live checklist. A mapping error flows into the build, the tests and the training, so the mapping is reviewed field by field before build starts. The standard is the same as the real work, because a bank that goes live with a wrong mapping fails its customers in seconds, in public, on the first day.
