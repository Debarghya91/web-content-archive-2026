What you will learn

Your objective. By the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain practical exercise: transform an MT103 into pacs.008. Here is a pre-migration MT103 we will transform, sent before coexistence ended on 22 November 2025.

Why it matters. This matters because the message is the payment instruction. A wrong field, a missing element or a misunderstood format is a failed payment, a return, or a compliance breach.

Chapter objective: Learner navigates the complete ISO 20022 catalogue, selecting the right message for each business scenario across payments, cash management, securities, and reporting. Learner practises this skill on realistic banking cases, explaining the reasoning clearly and using correct terminology throughout the work.

Key terms

Remittance information: Information that explains what a payment is for, such as invoice numbers, contract references or salary month.

Bank Operation Code: A code in field 23B of an MT103 that tells the receiving bank how to handle the transfer, such as CRED for a normal credit transfer, SPAY for a payment, or CRTS for a cheque related transfer.

Execution date: The date on which the bank executes a payment instruction, debiting the debtor account and sending the message onward.

Here is a pre-migration MT103 we will transform, sent before coexistence ended on 22 November 2025. A payment of GBP 12,500.00 from a customer in London to a supplier in New York. Field 20, the transaction reference, is FT20241105001. Field 23B is CRED for a credit transfer. Field 32A is 241106GBP12500,00, meaning value date 6 November 2024, currency GBP, amount 12,500.00.

Field 50K, the ordering customer, is Emma Wilson, 14 Oakfield Road, London NW3 2AB, United Kingdom, account GB29 NWBK 6016 1331 9268 19. Field 59, the beneficiary customer, is Hudson Trading LLC, 420 Madison Avenue, New York NY 10017, United States, account 987654321 with Chase Bank. Field 57A is the account with institution, CHASUS33XXX, and field 71A is SHA for shared charges. The MT103 continues with the details that explain the payment and its route. Field 70, remittance information, is INV-2024-0988 payment for consulting services October 2024.

Field 72, sender to receiver information, is PHONBEN plus 1 212 555 0148 please advise beneficiary by telephone. Field 52A, the ordering institution, is NWBKGB2LXXX for NatWest in London. Field 53B, the sender correspondent, is account 12345678 with the sending bank. Field 56A, the intermediary institution, is CHASUS33XXX for JPMorgan Chase in New York. The message was sent by NatWest in London on 5 November 2024, and it instructs Chase Bank to credit Hudson Trading LLC with GBP 12,500.00 for value 6 November 2024.

Mapping the header and amount fields is the most straightforward part of the transformation. Field 20, FT20241105001, becomes the instruction identifier in the pacs.008 payment identification, and a new message identifier such as MSG-2024-1105-009914 is created for the pacs.008 group header. Field 32A gives three pacs.008 values at once, the value date 2024-11-06 becomes the requested execution date and the interbank settlement date, the currency GBP becomes the instructed amount currency, and the amount 12,500.00 becomes the instructed amount.

Field 23B, CRED, is not carried as a field in pacs.008, because a pacs.008 is by definition a credit transfer, and the mapping must record that the value was consumed rather than lost. Mapping the parties and accounts needs care, because MT103 and pacs.008 structure names and addresses differently. Field 50K becomes the debtor in pacs.008, with the name Emma Wilson, the account GB29 NWBK 6016 1331 9268 19, and the address 14 Oakfield Road, London NW3 2AB, United Kingdom, split into street, town, postcode, and country code GB.

Field 59 becomes the creditor, with the name Hudson Trading LLC, the account 987654321, and the address 420 Madison Avenue, New York NY 10017, United States, with country code US. Field 57A, CHASUS33XXX, becomes the creditor agent, and field 52A, NWBKGB2LXXX, becomes the debtor agent. Field 56A, the intermediary, becomes the intermediary agent in pacs.008, a field that MT103 users sometimes overlook. Mapping the remaining fields shows where data loss and truncation risk live, and why the mapping must be checked line by line.

Field 71A, SHA, maps directly to the charge bearer SHAR in pacs.008, a clean one to one mapping. Field 70, INV-2024-0988 payment for consulting services October 2024, maps to the remittance information, and the invoice number INV-2024-0988 should also be copied to the end to end reference so Hudson Trading LLC can match the payment. Field 72, the telephone instruction, has no direct equivalent in pacs.008, so it must be carried as supplementary data or as an instruction for the creditor agent, and the mapping must record that decision.

Field 53B, the sender correspondent account, maps to the settlement information, not to a party field, and placing it in the wrong block would misdirect the settlement.
