Key terms

Settlement: The final movement of funds between banks to discharge obligations created by cleared payments. Settlement is usually made in central bank money or through correspondent accounts.

camt.056: An ISO 20022 message used to request cancellation of a payment instruction that was already sent. It is used for recalls, where the sender asks the receiving bank to return the funds.

camt.029: An ISO 20022 message that reports the resolution of an investigation, such as a cancellation request or a claim of non receipt.

Correct result. The message is made of blocks, each carrying a part of the payment instruction. The amount field and the identification field are the two a receiving bank checks first, because without them it cannot process or reconcile the payment.

Why. A message is only useful if you can read it. The blocks tell the receiving bank what to do, and the amount and identification fields are what it checks first.

A camt.056 is the FI to FI Payment Cancellation Request, and it is the message Meridian Bank Paris sends when Loire Components asks it to stop a payment that has already left the bank. Suppose the treasury team at Loire Components discovers on Tuesday morning that it sent the EUR 48,250 payment twice, once by file and once by online banking, and it asks Meridian Bank Paris to cancel the duplicate. Meridian Bank Paris sends a camt.056.001.08 to Harbour Bank Frankfurt, naming the original pacs.008, the end to end identification LOIRE-RHINE-2026-1148, and the reason for the cancellation request.

The camt.056 asks for action, but it does not move money by itself. Harbour Bank Frankfurt answers the cancellation request with a camt.029.001.09 Resolution of Investigation message, and that answer decides what happens next. If the duplicate payment has not yet been credited to Rhine Metals, Harbour Bank Frankfurt can stop it and reply with a positive camt.029, confirming the cancellation. If the funds have already been credited, the bank cannot simply take them back, and it replies with a negative camt.029, explaining that the payment has already been settled to the beneficiary.

The camt.029 carries a status and a reason code, so Meridian Bank Paris knows whether to expect a return or to start a different recovery process. When a cancellation succeeds, the funds come back through a pacs.004 Payment Return, and the return is a new settlement instruction in its own right. Harbour Bank Frankfurt sends a pacs.004.001.09 for EUR 48,250, carrying the original end to end identification, the return reason code, and the reference to the original pacs.008. The clearing system settles the return in the next exchange period, Meridian Bank Paris receives the funds, and it credits EUR 48,250 back to the Loire Components account.

Meridian Bank Paris then sends a pain.002 report to Loire Components confirming the return, so the treasury team can reconcile the duplicate and close the investigation. Timing decides whether a camt.056 can succeed, and a business analyst must design the process around that fact. A cancellation request sent within minutes of the original payment has a good chance of catching the payment before the beneficiary bank credits it. A request sent a day later, after Rhine Metals has already used the funds to pay its own suppliers, will almost certainly receive a negative camt.029.

In that case, the recovery depends on Rhine Metals agreeing to return the money voluntarily, and Meridian Bank Paris may need to send a new instruction or a request for a refund. The SCT scheme rules set time limits for these steps, and the banks must follow them.
