Key terms

Settlement: The final movement of funds between banks to discharge obligations created by cleared payments. Settlement is usually made in central bank money or through correspondent accounts.

Value date: The date on which funds become available to the beneficiary and on which interest starts to accrue. It is the date used for settlement and for nostro posting.

ISO 20022: An international standard for financial messaging that defines XML message formats for payments, securities and trade.

  1. What to do. List the main blocks of the message, name the field that carries the amount, and name the field that identifies the payment.

Correct result. The message is made of blocks, each carrying a part of the payment instruction. The amount field and the identification field are the two a receiving bank checks first, because without them it cannot process or reconcile the payment.

Why. A message is only useful if you can read it. The blocks tell the receiving bank what to do, and the amount and identification fields are what it checks first.

Transcript and full text

Show transcript

Every ISO 20022 message name has three parts, and each part carries meaning. The four letter prefix names the business area. The prefix pain means payment initiation, and those messages live in customer to bank space. The prefix pacs means payments clearing and settlement, and those messages live in interbank space. The prefix camt means cash management, and those messages carry statements, reports and investigations.

Once you know the prefix, you know which space the message belongs to, which parties exchange it, and roughly what it does, before you read a single field inside it. The three digit number after the prefix identifies the message within its business area. In the pain family, 001 is a customer credit transfer initiation and 002 is a customer payment status report, while 008 is a customer direct debit initiation. In the pacs family, 002 is a payment status report, 003 is a direct debit collection, 004 is a payment return, 007 is a reversal, and 008 is a customer credit transfer.

In the camt family, 052 is a bank to customer account report, 053 is a bank to customer statement, 054 is a debit or credit notification, 056 is a cancellation request, and 029 is a resolution of investigation. The version number at the end of the name identifies the message definition version, and it matters because fields change between versions. The name pacs.008.001.08 means the pacs.008 message, variant 001, version 08. The name pain.001.001.09 means the pain.001 message, variant 001, version 09.

A bank that expects version 09 will reject a file built to version 03, because mandatory fields may be missing or structured differently. When a business analyst writes a requirement, the full name with its version must be stated, or developers and testers may build against the wrong definition and fail certification testing. One business event can generate several message names across the spaces, and the worked example shows how. Loire Components sends a pain.001.001.09 to Meridian Bank Paris to start the EUR 48,250 payment.

Meridian Bank Paris replies with a pain.002.001.10 status report, then creates a pacs.008.001.08 for the interbank leg to Harbour Bank Frankfurt. Harbour Bank Frankfurt may reply with a pacs.002.001.10 status report, and it will report the credit to Rhine Metals with a camt.053.001.08 statement. The same EUR 48,250 appears in four messages, but each message serves a different audience and a different purpose.
