Confirmation of Payee, known as CoP, is a service that checks whether the name entered by the payer matches the name on the account that the sort code and account number identify, and it is now a standard part of the UK payment journey. When a customer sets up a new payee, the bank sends the name, sort code and account number to the payee bank, which returns one of four responses, the name matches, the name is a close match and the correct name is shown, the name does not match, or the account cannot be checked. The payer then decides whether to proceed.

A no match response is a strong warning sign, because it often means the sort code or account number was typed wrong, or the payer is being tricked into paying a fraudster. The reimbursement rules for authorised push payment fraud are a second layer of protection that the analyst must understand. Under the rules set by the Payment Systems Regulator, banks must reimburse victims of authorised push payment fraud in defined circumstances, with the cost shared between the sending and receiving banks.

The rules have exceptions, for example where the customer acted with gross negligence or where the payment was to an account the customer controlled, but the general principle is that the victim is protected. This changes the economics of fraud for banks. Preventing a fraudulent payment is cheaper than reimbursing it, so banks invest heavily in warnings, in checks, and in slowing down payments that look risky. CoP also shows how a payment system evolves to meet a new threat. Faster Payments was built for speed, and speed made fraud harder to stop, because a payment that settles in seconds cannot be recalled.

The industry responded not by slowing the system down, but by adding a check before the payment is sent, so that the customer has the information they need to make a good decision. The lesson for the analyst is that fraud prevention belongs at the start of the flow, not at the end. A warning shown after the money has gone is useless; a warning shown before the customer confirms the payment can save them. The best payment designs make the safe choice the easy choice, and CoP is a good example of that principle in action.
