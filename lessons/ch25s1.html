What a Payment Hub Is and Why Banks Build One

Payment Hub Architecture, Lesson 1 of 8, Narration 2:04, D09 Payment architecture and technology

Note saved.

What you will learn

Your objective. By the end of this lesson, you will be able to explain payment hub. A payment hub is a central platform that processes many different payment types through one shared set of services, instead of each product having its own separate system.

Why it matters. This matters because the architecture decides what the bank can build, how fast it can change, and where a payment can fail. Most payment requirements live in the architecture.

Chapter objective: Learner designs a payment hub architecture, explaining intake, validation, routing, transformation, screening, and settlement modules, and maps a payment through each module. Learner applies this skill in realistic banking scenarios, with clear examples and correct terminology throughout.

Key learning points

  • Payment hub components and contents (master lesson ch48s1)
  • Payment hub
  • Hub versus gateway distinction
  • Read ch48s1 for the full treatment

Key terms

Payment hub: A central system in a bank that receives payment instructions from all channels, validates them, applies screening and routing rules, and sends them to the correct clearing system or correspondent.

Clearing: The process of exchanging payment instructions between banks and calculating what each bank owes the others.

Gateway: A service that connects a merchant website or app to payment networks and acquirers. It encrypts card data, routes authorisation requests, and returns the result to the merchant.

Practise this lesson

Exercise

Read ch48s1 Hub components: what a hub is and what it contains. Then explain in your own words why a bank with five payment silos builds a hub.

ch48s1 covers the hub definition, the five layers, the gateway, the routing decision, and the business case. This lesson is a pointer only.

  1. What to do. Write three sentences: what a hub is, which duplicated functions it removes, and what single view it gives the bank.

Correct result. A payment hub is a central platform that processes payments from every channel through one set of common functions. It removes the duplicate screening, limits and reporting that five silos each run separately. It gives the bank one view of every payment from instruction to settlement.

Why. The point of the hub is consolidation, not speed. One screening check, one limit check, one routing decision, one view.
