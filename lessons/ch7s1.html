Key terms

Remittance information: Information that explains what a payment is for, such as invoice numbers, contract references or salary month.

Reconciliation: The process of comparing records from two sources, such as a bank statement and an internal ledger, to confirm they agree.

Charge bearer: A code that states who pays the charges for a payment. In ISO 20022 the values are DEBT for debtor, CRED for creditor, SHAR for shared and SLEV for service level.

Correct result. The amount is carried in the instructed amount element and the reference in the end-to-end identification. The three levels are the group header, the payment information, and the credit transfer transaction information.

Why. A message is only useful if you can read it. The group header identifies the message, the payment information identifies the batch, and the transaction information carries the single payment.

Show transcript

The pacs.008 message, whose full name is FIToFICustomerCreditTransfer, is the ISO 20022 message used for a customer credit transfer between financial institutions. It is the direct successor to the SWIFT MT103, and it is used whenever a debtor agent sends a customer payment to a creditor agent, either directly or through intermediary banks. The message carries the debtor and creditor details, the amount and currency, the value date, the charges, and the remittance information, just as an MT103 does, but it carries them in XML with far richer structure and with more room for detail.

It is the workhorse message for cross border customer payments under CBPR Plus. A pacs.008 can contain one or more transactions, grouped under one or more Payment Information blocks. In practice, many banks send one transaction per message for cross border payments, to make tracking and exception handling simpler. A bulk pacs.008 with many transactions is possible, but it requires careful handling, because a single error in one transaction should not cause the entire message to be rejected. The message is sent through the SWIFT network or through a domestic clearing system that has adopted ISO 20022, and it is validated against the CBPR Plus usage guideline before it is accepted.

The pacs.008 is used for supplier payments, salary payments, and any other customer credit transfer where the originator is not a bank. It is not used for bank to bank payments, which use pacs.009, and it is not used for direct debits, which use pacs.003. An analyst must be able to distinguish those three messages, because they have different structures, different mandatory fields, and different business meanings. Confusing a pacs.008 with a pacs.009 is a common error in requirements and in testing, and it can cause a payment to be processed with the wrong charges, the wrong screening, or the wrong reconciliation.
