Key terms

Correspondent banking: A service where one bank holds accounts for another bank so the second bank can make payments in currencies or countries where it has no branch.

pacs.008: An ISO 20022 message for a financial institution to financial institution customer credit transfer. It replaces MT103 in cross border payments and carries structured debtor, creditor, charges and...

MT103: A SWIFT message that carries a single customer credit transfer instruction from one bank to another. It names the ordering customer, the beneficiary, the amount, the currency and the value date.

Exercise

Read the MT103 serial message described in this lesson and identify its key parts.

Use the message structure, fields and example in this lesson.

  1. What to do. List the main blocks of the message, name the field that carries the amount, and name the field that identifies the payment.

Correct result. The message is made of blocks, each carrying a part of the payment instruction. The amount field and the identification field are the two a receiving bank checks first, because without them it cannot process or reconcile the payment.

Why. A message is only useful if you can read it. The blocks tell the receiving bank what to do, and the amount and identification fields are what it checks first.

This worked example is set before the CBPR+ migration, so the payment travels as MT103s. The same payment, USD 367,574.90 from a French company at BNP Paribas to a Spanish company at Santander, can settle by the serial method instead. In the serial method only one message type is used, the MT103 serial. But the message is sent three times, once between each pair of banks in the chain, and each time its content changes slightly. Following these changes is the best way to understand how serial payments really work. The same payment today travels as a chain of pacs.008 messages, one between each pair of banks. The first MT103 serial goes from BNP Paribas (BNPAFRPP) to Wells Fargo (PNBPUS3N). BNP Paribas instructs Wells Fargo to debit its account and credit the intermediary institution, Bank of New York Mellon (IRVTUS3N). Fields 53A and 54A are absent, because the payment does not go through correspondents in the MT sense. BNP Paribas has a direct account with Wells Fargo. Field 52A is absent too, which means the ordering customer is a customer of BNP Paribas itself. Field 57A is present, naming Santander (BSCHESMM) as the account with institution, because the beneficiary account is not held by Wells Fargo but by Santander. Charges are SHA. The second MT103 serial goes from Wells Fargo (PNBPUS3N) to Bank of New York Mellon (IRVTUS3N). Wells Fargo instructs Bank of New York Mellon to debit its account and credit Santander's account. Two things change. First, field 52A is now present, naming BNP Paribas as the ordering institution. The ordering customer is not a customer of Wells Fargo, so Wells Fargo adds this field so the next bank knows who really ordered the payment. Second, Wells Fargo deducts its charges. A new field 71F appears with the value USD20. The interbank settled amount in field 32A is now USD 367,554.90, which is the instructed amount of USD 367,574.90 in field 33B minus the USD 20 charges. The sender must provide field 33B whenever charges have been deducted, and also because field 33B was present in the MT103 it received. The third MT103 serial goes from Bank of New York Mellon (IRVTUS3N) to Santander (BSCHESMM). Its meaning is different from the first two. Bank of New York Mellon is informing Santander that its account has been credited and the funds are for the beneficiary customer, so Santander should credit the beneficiary account. It is a notification of credit, not an instruction to debit. Field 52A is still present, carried forward from the previous message. Field 57A is now absent, because the beneficiary account is held by Santander itself, the receiver of this message, so there is no need to name an account with institution. A second field 71F is added with the value USD22, Bank of New York Mellon's charges. The message now carries two 71F fields, USD20 and USD22. The interbank settled amount is USD 367,532.90, which is the instructed amount of USD 367,574.90 minus USD20 minus USD22. Compare the two methods. In the cover method, the announcement and the cover travel separately, and the beneficiary bank may credit its customer before the funds arrive, depending on trust, amount, and relationship. In the serial method, each bank only acts when it receives the message and the funds together, so there is no gap between instruction and money. That is why the serial method is often called the American method and the cover method the European method. The serial method is preferred in the United States, the cover method prevails in Europe. A business analyst who can trace both methods through the same payment, field by field, understands correspondent banking at a level most practitioners never reach.
