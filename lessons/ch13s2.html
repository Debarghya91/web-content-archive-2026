Transcript and full text

Show transcript

Nostro and vostro are the two words that confuse every new analyst in cross border payments, and the confusion disappears once the perspective is clear. Nostro means our account with you, and vostro means your account with us, and they describe the same account from opposite sides. When Bank A in India opens a US dollar account with Bank B in New York, Bank A calls that account its nostro account, because it is our account held with you. Bank B calls the same account its vostro account, because it is your account held with us.

The account is one account, held on the books of Bank B, and the two names simply record which bank is speaking. Every nostro account is a vostro account for the other bank, and every vostro account is a nostro account for the other bank. The distinction matters because the accounting is different on each side. On the books of Bank A, the nostro account is an asset. It is money that Bank A owns and holds with Bank B, and it appears as a balance that Bank A can use to make payments.

On the books of Bank B, the vostro account is a liability. It is money that Bank B owes to Bank A, and it appears as a balance that Bank B must be ready to pay out when Bank A instructs a payment. When Bank A sends a payment from its nostro account, Bank A reduces its asset and Bank B reduces its liability by the same amount. When money arrives into the nostro account, Bank A increases its asset and Bank B increases its liability. A simple example makes the idea concrete.

Suppose State Bank of India holds a US dollar nostro account with JPMorgan Chase in New York, and the balance is ten million dollars. State Bank of India records ten million dollars as an asset in its own books. JPMorgan Chase records ten million dollars as a liability in its books, owed to State Bank of India. Now a customer of State Bank of India wants to pay one million dollars to a supplier in the United States. State Bank of India instructs JPMorgan Chase to pay one million dollars from the nostro account.

JPMorgan Chase debits the vostro account, reducing the liability to nine million dollars, and sends the payment through Fedwire or CHIPS to the supplier's bank. State Bank of India records the nostro balance as nine million dollars. The two banks must agree on the balance at the end of each day, and any difference is a reconciliation break that must be investigated. That daily agreement is the heart of nostro reconciliation.
