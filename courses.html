Payment Hub vs Payment Gateway vs Payment Platform

A payment gateway sits at the front: it takes a payment from a channel, tokenises and screens it, and hands it on. A payment hub is the enterprise engine inside the bank: it validates, enriches, screens, routes, processes, clears, settles and reconciles every payment type across every rail. A payment platform is the whole enterprise design: hub plus gateways, channels, core banking, ledger, APIs and operations. Component-level design lives in the hub chapter; enterprise-level design lives in the platform chapter.

Payment Gateway Architecture → Payment Hub Architecture → Payment Hub: Enterprise Architecture → Payment Platform Architecture and Your Career → System Design Deep Dive →

Liquidity Management and Nostro / Vostro

Liquidity management and nostro/vostro accounts are separate chapters that cross-reference each other: liquidity is about having the right funds at the right moment, and nostro accounts are where those funds sit for cross-border payments.

Liquidity Management → Correspondent Banking and Nostro Vostro →

Cross-Border Payments and FEMA

Cross-border payment processing and FEMA compliance are linked but different: processing moves the money, FEMA decides whether the movement is permitted and what must be reported. Learn the processing first, then the regulation that governs it.

Correspondent Banking and Nostro Vostro → Indian Payments Deep Dive: NACH, BBPS, FEMA →
