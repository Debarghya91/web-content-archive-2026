Payments Training Hub is sold as courses. The integrated course is the whole academy. Smaller bundles cover one area each. A bundle unlocks only its own chapters, so pick the area you need, or take everything with the integrated course.

How the curriculum fits together

Four structures organise the same 99 chapters, and one more organises the situations product. The 15 domains are the subject map: each domain is one area of payments, and every chapter belongs to one domain. The 14 learning stages are the order to learn in: each stage is one learning goal, the stages run in order, stages 1 to 13 teach the subject, and stage 14 is six capstone projects where you prove it. The 8-session learning path is a guided route through selected chapters: follow the sessions in order, and sessions 7 and 8 are hands-on, with requirements, user stories, mappings and test cases. Each bundle groups chapters by area: the integrated course covers all 15 domains, and each smaller bundle covers the chapters of its area. Payment Situations is included in the Integrated Course and is also available on its own: its 564 items run in six stages of their own, from payment mechanics to senior SME judgement.

Start here: your 8-session guided path

This is the default route for new learners. Follow the sessions in order, from payment fundamentals to the hands-on BA workshop and the capstone. Each session maps to the chapters below it. If you already know where you need to go, the bundles and the other maps are further down this page. For every path with prerequisites, hours and outcomes, see the Course map.

Your 8-Session Learning Path

Follow the sessions in order. Each session maps to the chapters below. Sessions 7 and 8 are hands-on: you write requirements, user stories, mappings, and test cases, the way a payments BA does on a real project.

Session 01: Payment Fundamentals and Ecosystem

Global payments landscape, ecosystem and key stakeholders, domestic, cross-border, SEPA, instant and card payments, overview of payment systems and business scenarios.

What is a Payment → Payment Instruments and Types → The Payment Ecosystem → Payment Lifecycle End to End → Cheques and Paper Instruments → Card Payments Deep Dive →

Session 02: Payment Lifecycle and Processing

End-to-end payment lifecycle, initiation, clearing and settlement, domestic and cross-border process flows, key payment events and exception scenarios.

Payment Lifecycle End to End → Clearing Concepts and Netting → Settlement Models → Cross-Border Exceptions → Payment Exceptions and Investigations → Trade Finance Deep Dive →

Session 03: ISO 20022 and SWIFT Messaging

Introduction to SWIFT and ISO 20022, message structure and important fields, key messages pacs.008, pacs.009, pacs.002, pacs.004, business purpose and interpretation.

SWIFT MT Messages → ISO 20022 Fundamentals → pacs.008 Deep Dive → Complete ISO 20022 Catalogue → pain.001 and pain.002 in Depth → ISO 20022 Technical Implementation →

Session 04: CBPR+ and Payment Message Mapping

CBPR+ framework and ISO 20022 migration, SWIFT MT103 to ISO 20022 pacs.008 mapping, business events that trigger payment messages, message fields, validations and real-world scenarios.

MT to MX Migration and CBPR+ → Capstone Project → Payment Data Model and Mapping →

Session 05: SEPA Schemes and Instant Payments

SEPA Credit Transfer and SEPA Direct Debit, SEPA Instant Payments and UPI overview, scheme rules, processing timelines and cut-off times, business requirements, compliance and exception handling.

Europe: SEPA and T2 → India: UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS → US: Fedwire, FedNow, ACH, CHIPS → UK: Faster Payments, CHAPS, BACS → TARGET Services: T2, T2S and TIPS → TIPS in Depth: Instant Payments and Liquidity Transfers → SEPA Credit Transfer Deep Dive → SEPA Direct Debit Deep Dive →

Session 06: Payment Controls, Compliance and Operations

KYC, AML and transaction monitoring from a BA perspective, sanctions screening, PEPs and watchlists, fraud detection concepts and risk-based rules, payment exceptions, investigations and repair, accounting, reconciliation and operational controls.

Sanctions Screening → AML, KYC and Fraud Prevention → Liquidity Management → Settlement Risk → Payment Reconciliation → Reject and Return in CBPR+ → Open Banking, PSD2 and PSD3 → Payment Operations Architecture: State, Resilience and Control →

Session 07: Business Analyst Practical Workshop

Requirements gathering and business process modelling, As-Is and To-Be payment process flows, business rules and processing conditions, Jira user stories and acceptance criteria, introduction to API analysis and interface specifications.

BA Practical Workshop → Banking Integration Patterns →

Session 08: End-to-End BA Simulation and Capstone Project

SWIFT MT103 to ISO 20022 pacs.008 message mapping, API business data, rules and validations, user stories and acceptance criteria, UAT scenarios and end-to-end test cases, hands-on BA deliverables and capstone exercises.

Capstone Project → Payment Testing and Certification →

Guided Learning Tracks

Guided Learning Tracks

Some topics span several chapters. Follow a track in order and the pieces connect.

India Payments Track: One Comprehensive Track

India is covered as a single track, from everyday rails to the deep mechanics. Start with the rails, then go deep on mandates, bill payments and regulation.

India: UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS → Indian Payments Deep Dive: NACH, BBPS, FEMA →

Card Payments Track: One Complete Track

Card processing and card networks are one track: how a card payment is authorised, cleared and settled, how the networks move the messages, and how wallets and POS fit in.

Card Authorization, Clearing and Settlement → 3D Secure, Tokenization and Chargebacks → Card Networks: Visa and Mastercard → Digital Wallets and POS Systems →

Payment Hub vs Payment Gateway vs Payment Platform

A payment gateway sits at the front: it takes a payment from a channel, tokenises and screens it, and hands it on. A payment hub is the enterprise engine inside the bank: it validates, enriches, screens, routes, processes, clears, settles and reconciles every payment type across every rail. A payment platform is the whole enterprise design: hub plus gateways, channels, core banking, ledger, APIs and operations. Component-level design lives in the hub chapter; enterprise-level design lives in the platform chapter.

Payment Gateway Architecture → Payment Hub Architecture → Payment Hub: Enterprise Architecture → Payment Platform Architecture and Your Career → System Design Deep Dive →

Liquidity Management and Nostro / Vostro

Liquidity management and nostro/vostro accounts are separate chapters that cross-reference each other: liquidity is about having the right funds at the right moment, and nostro accounts are where those funds sit for cross-border payments.

Liquidity Management → Correspondent Banking and Nostro Vostro →

Cross-Border Payments and FEMA

Cross-border payment processing and FEMA compliance are linked but different: processing moves the money, FEMA decides whether the movement is permitted and what must be reported. Learn the processing first, then the regulation that governs it.

Correspondent Banking and Nostro Vostro → Indian Payments Deep Dive: NACH, BBPS, FEMA →
