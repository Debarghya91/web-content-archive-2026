camt.056: FI to FI Payment Cancellation Request

camt.056 is the ISO 20022 message one bank sends to another to request cancellation of a payment instruction it already sent. It is used when the customer asks to stop a payment, when the payment was sent in error, or when fraud is suspected. It carries the original payment reference and a coded cancellation reason. Cancellation is not guaranteed once the payment has been executed or settled.

At a glance

  • Message: camt.056
  • Title: FI to FI Payment Cancellation Request
  • Current version: camt.056.001.08
  • Versions: camt.056.001.01, camt.056.001.08
  • Fields: 12 (4 mandatory, 8 optional)

Participants

The sending participant is the bank that sent the original payment and now wants it stopped, usually the debtor agent. The receiving participant is the bank holding the payment, usually the creditor agent or an intermediary agent.

Structure

camt.056 is an ISO 20022 XML message with an Assignment block (Assgnmt) followed by an Underlying block (Undrlyg) that identifies the original payment. It carries the original instruction, end to end and transaction identifications, the original settlement amount and date, CancellationReasonInformation (CxlRsnInf) and, where needed, a Case block for the investigation.

Scheme rules

camt.056 is a base ISO 20022 message: the structure and fields below are the base definition, and each scheme adds its own rules for its rail. Under SEPA a recall may only use the reasons DUPL, TECH or FRAD, and the creditor bank must answer within the SEPA recall timetable. Under CBPR+ a cancellation is a request, not an instruction: the receiving bank is not obliged to cancel a payment that has already been executed or settled.

Fields

FieldNameMandatoryDescription
AssgnmtAssignmentMThe assignment block holding the identification of the cancellation request, the assigner and the assignee. It identifies the request for tracking and audit. The assigner is the bank requesting cancellation and the assignee is the bank that received the original payment instruction.
UndrlygUnderlyingMThe underlying transaction block holding the original payment reference and details. It tells the receiving bank exactly which payment the cancellation refers to. It carries the original instruction identification, end to end identification and transaction identification from the pacs.008 or pacs.009.
OrgnlGrpInfOriginal Group InformationOInformation about the original message group, including its message identification and creation date. It helps the receiving bank find the original message in its archive. Accurate group information speeds the search, especially when the bank processes many payments from the same sender each day.
OrgnlInstrIdOriginal Instruction IdentificationMThe instruction identification of the original payment, copied into the cancellation request. It is the primary key the receiving bank uses to find the payment. A wrong instruction identification means the bank may cancel the wrong payment or fail to find the payment at all.
OrgnlEndToEndIdOriginal End to End IdentificationOThe end to end identification of the original payment, copied into the cancellation request. It stays unchanged through the chain and lets all parties refer to the same payment. It is the reference the customer uses when asking the bank to stop a payment it already instructed.
OrgnlTxIdOriginal Transaction IdentificationOThe transaction identification of the original payment, assigned by the instructing agent. It gives the receiving bank a further matching key. Banks use it to confirm the cancellation refers to the correct transaction when instruction identification alone is not unique in their systems.
OrgnlIntrBkSttlmAmtOriginal Interbank Settlement AmountOThe interbank settlement amount of the original payment, copied into the request. It confirms which payment is being cancelled when several payments share similar references. The receiving bank compares it with its own record before acting on the cancellation request.
OrgnlIntrBkSttlmDtOriginal Interbank Settlement DateOThe interbank settlement date of the original payment, copied into the request. It confirms the value date of the payment to be cancelled. The receiving bank uses it to judge whether the payment is still cancellable or has already settled and therefore needs a return instead.
CxlRsnInfCancellation Reason InformationMThe reason for the cancellation, with a coded reason and additional text. Codes include DUPL for duplicate payment, FRAD for fraud, CUST for customer request and TECH for technical error. The reason tells the receiving bank why the payment must stop and supports its audit and reporting.
OrgnlTxRefOriginal Transaction ReferenceOReference data from the original payment, such as amount, currency, debtor and creditor, copied into the request. It helps the receiving bank match the cancellation when the identification fields alone are not enough. It also gives operations staff the details to verify the request.
CaseCaseOCase information for the cancellation, including the case identification and the creator of the case. It links the cancellation request to an investigation or customer query case in the requesting bank. The case reference is used in later status messages about the cancellation outcome.
CtrlDataControl DataOControl data for the cancellation request, such as the number of transactions covered. It is used when one request covers several payments. The receiving bank checks the control data against the number of underlying transactions to confirm it received the complete request.

Valid example

Meridian Bank London sends a camt.056.001.08 to Harbour Bank Singapore to cancel the pacs.008 with end to end identification MER-20261105-1187, reason DUPL, duplicate payment sent twice by the customer. Harbour Bank confirms the payment has not yet been credited and stops it.

Invalid example

A camt.056 arrives asking to cancel a payment that settled three days ago, with reason code CUST and no original transaction identification. The receiving bank cannot find the payment and cannot cancel a settled payment, so it rejects the request (error: payment already settled).

Validation errors

  • Payment already settled: a cancellation request for a payment that has been executed or credited cannot be honoured and is rejected.
  • Invalid cancellation reason under SEPA: a SEPA recall with a reason other than DUPL, TECH or FRAD fails scheme validation.
  • Unknown original instruction identification: a request that quotes an OrgnlInstrId the receiving bank never saw cannot be matched to any payment.

Processing notes

The receiving bank checks whether the payment is still held, stops it if it has not been credited, and replies with a camt.029 stating whether the cancellation was accepted or rejected. If the payment has already been credited, the debtor agent must ask the creditor to return the funds instead, usually by a pacs.004.

Accounting notes

A camt.056 posts no accounting entries itself; it only requests cancellation. If the payment is stopped before posting, nothing is booked. If the payment was already posted, the return is booked as a separate credit entry, and the original debit stays on the ledger for the audit trail.

Related messages

pacs.008, pacs.004, camt.029, MT103
