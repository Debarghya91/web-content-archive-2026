camt.029: Resolution of Investigation

camt.029 is the ISO 20022 message a bank sends to resolve an investigation into a payment. It answers a claim raised about a missing, duplicated or wrongly credited payment and states whether the claim is accepted or rejected. It carries the case reference, the original payment details and the resolution outcome. Banks use it to close payment investigations and to agree any return of funds.

At a glance

  • Message: camt.029
  • Title: Resolution of Investigation
  • Current version: camt.029.001.09
  • Versions: camt.029.001.03, camt.029.001.07, camt.029.001.08, camt.029.001.09
  • Fields: 12 (5 mandatory, 7 optional)

Participants

The sending participant is the bank that investigated the payment dispute. The receiving participant is the bank that raised the investigation, which needs the outcome to inform its customer and close the case.

Structure

camt.029 has an assignment block with the resolution identification, a case block with the investigation reference, a status block with the outcome and reason, and an underlying block with the original payment details. The document outline follows the standard ISO 20022 investigation structure with one resolution per message.

Scheme rules

camt.029 is the base ISO 20022 investigation resolution message, used across CBPR+ and domestic schemes with the same structure. CBPR+ requires the resolution within the scheme investigation time limit, while domestic schemes may set shorter limits. The message itself carries no scheme specific party rules, since it reports an outcome rather than instructing a payment.

Fields

FieldNameMandatoryDescription
AssgnmtAssignmentMThe assignment block holding the identification of the resolution, the assigner and the assignee. It identifies the investigation response for tracking and audit. The assigner is the bank that investigated the case and the assignee is the bank that raised the original investigation.
CaseCaseMThe case information for the investigation, including the case identification and the creator of the case. It links the resolution to the investigation raised in a camt.028 or a claim. The case reference is used in later messages about the same payment dispute.
StsStatusMThe status of the investigation, using codes such as RJCR for rejected, ACCP for accepted, or PDNG for pending. It tells the requesting bank whether its claim was accepted. The status drives the next action, whether that is to return the funds, to close the case, or to escalate it.
UndrlygUnderlyingMThe original payment reference and details for the payment under investigation. It carries the original instruction identification, end to end identification and transaction identification. It tells the receiving bank exactly which payment the resolution refers to in its systems.
OrgnlInstrIdOriginal Instruction IdentificationMThe instruction identification of the original payment, copied into the resolution. It is the primary key the requesting bank uses to find the payment. A wrong instruction identification means the resolution may be applied to the wrong payment or fail to match at all.
OrgnlEndToEndIdOriginal End to End IdentificationOThe end to end identification of the original payment, copied into the resolution. It stays unchanged through the chain and lets all parties refer to the same payment. It is the reference the customer uses when asking about the outcome of the investigation.
OrgnlTxIdOriginal Transaction IdentificationOThe transaction identification of the original payment, assigned by the instructing agent. It gives the receiving bank a further matching key. Banks use it to confirm the resolution refers to the correct transaction when instruction identification alone is not unique in their systems.
OrgnlIntrBkSttlmAmtOriginal Interbank Settlement AmountOThe interbank settlement amount of the original payment, copied into the resolution. It confirms which payment is being resolved when several payments share similar references. The receiving bank compares it with its own record before acting on the resolution outcome.
RsltnInfResolution InformationOThe resolution outcome details, including whether the payment will be returned, corrected, or left as settled. It can carry the return amount and the value date of the return. The requesting bank uses it to inform the customer and to post any agreed return to the account.
StsRsnInfStatus Reason InformationOThe reason for the resolution outcome, with a coded reason and additional free text. Codes explain why a claim was accepted or rejected, such as no error found or payment already credited. Clear reason codes let the requesting bank explain the outcome to its customer.
ChrgsInfCharges InformationOCharges related to the investigation, when the resolving bank applies a fee for its work. It can show the amount and bearer of the fee. The requesting bank uses it to reconcile investigation charges against its fee schedule and to query any charge it does not recognise.
CtrlDataControl DataOControl data for the resolution, such as the number of transactions covered. It is used when one resolution covers several payments in the same case. The receiving bank checks the control data against the number of underlying transactions to confirm it received the complete resolution.

Valid example

Banco Santander Madrid sends a camt.029 to BNP Paribas Paris resolving case INV55210 about a payment of USD 367,574.90 that the customer said was missing. Santander confirms the funds were credited to the beneficiary on 6 November 2026, rejects the claim with reason no error found, and BNP Paribas informs the customer and closes the case.

Invalid example

A camt.029 arrives with a case identification that matches no open investigation and no original instruction identification. The requesting bank cannot link the resolution to any payment it queried, so it rejects the message and asks the resolving bank to resend it with the correct case and payment references.

Validation errors

  • Case identification matching no open investigation causes rejection because the resolution cannot be linked to any payment dispute.
  • Original instruction identification missing causes a matching failure because the bank cannot confirm which payment the resolution refers to.
  • Status code invalid or missing causes a hold because the receiving bank cannot tell whether the claim was accepted or rejected.

Processing notes

The receiving bank matches the resolution to its open case by case identification and original payment reference. Accepted claims trigger a return or correction of the payment, while rejected claims close the case with the reason given. The outcome is reported to the customer, and the case is archived with the resolution attached.

Accounting notes

The camt.029 itself posts nothing, since it only reports the outcome of an investigation. The underlying payment entries were already posted when the original payment settled. A return agreed in the resolution posts a reverse entry, while a rejection leaves the original entries unchanged and the investigation is closed.
