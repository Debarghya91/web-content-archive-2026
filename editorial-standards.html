Editorial Standards

How the content on Payments Training Hub is written, checked, and corrected.

Sources first

Every lesson starts from the authoritative documentation for its subject: the scheme rulebooks, message standards, and official guidance listed on the Sources page. A lesson states what a rule or message does, shows it with a worked example, and explains the failure modes and the operational consequences, so the reader can check the claim against the source.

What counts as correct

A statement is correct when it matches the current scheme documentation or the documented behaviour of the system it describes. Where schemes differ, the lesson says which scheme it is describing. Where a rule has changed, the lesson is updated to the current version and the chapter review date is updated with it.

Review

Chapters carry a version and a last-reviewed date where that information is published on the chapter page. Content is re-checked against its sources when a scheme publishes a new rulebook version, and corrections reported by readers are checked against the source and fixed.

What this site does not do

This site does not publish testimonials, student numbers, success rates, salary claims, or endorsements. Counts on the site (lessons, chapters, questions, simulators) are counted from the published curriculum, not estimated. No lesson claims a partnership, accreditation, or affiliation that does not exist.

Corrections

If you find an error, report it through the Contact page with the page address and the source that shows the correct position. Verified corrections are fixed and the review date is updated.
