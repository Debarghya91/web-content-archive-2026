Domains

The curriculum is organised into 15 domains. Each domain is a subject area with its own chapters and lessons. Start at Domain 01 if you are new to payments, or jump to the domain you need.

Domain 01: Foundations of money and payments5 chapters · 34 lessons. Start here. This domain explains what a payment is, what money is, and how the pieces fit together before any message or system is introduced. It covers payment instruments, the parties in the ecosystem, paper instruments and cheques, and the types of money that change how a payment works. Domain 02: Accounts, ledger and accounting3 chapters · 19 lessons. Every payment ends as an entry in a ledger. This domain explains the accounts a bank holds, what a balance means, how double-entry bookkeeping records a payment, and how a bank’s ledger systems keep those records consistent. Domain 03: Payment lifecycle2 chapters · 14 lessons. A payment is a journey with a beginning, a middle and an end. This domain follows it from initiation to final settlement, including the timelines for instant, batch and cross-border payments and the failure modes at each stage. Domain 04: Messaging and standards12 chapters · 97 lessons. Payments run on messages. This domain teaches the two message families that carry them, SWIFT MT and ISO 20022, field by field, and how the industry is moving from one to the other under CBPR+. Domain 05: Domestic payment systems15 chapters · 113 lessons. Every country settles payments differently. This domain covers the domestic systems of India, the United States, Europe and the United Kingdom, and the instant payment systems now running around the world. Domain 06: Cross-border payments10 chapters · 75 lessons. When a payment crosses a border, it passes through correspondent banks, nostro accounts and several intermediaries. This domain explains how that chain works, what it costs, how it is tracked, and what happens when it breaks. Domain 07: Clearing, settlement and liquidity7 chapters · 49 lessons. Clearing sorts payments, settlement moves the money, and liquidity funds it all. This domain explains netting, the settlement models, how banks manage intraday liquidity, and the risks that settlement creates. Domain 08: Cards and alternative payments7 chapters · 52 lessons. A card payment is a chain of authorisation, clearing and settlement that involves the merchant, the acquirer, the network and the issuer. This domain follows that chain and covers wallets, recurring payments and the compliance that surrounds them. Domain 09: Payment architecture and technology7 chapters · 53 lessons. Behind every payment sits a system that has to be fast, correct and always available. This domain explains how payment hubs, gateways and engines are built, how data flows through them, and how they are observed in production. Domain 10: APIs and integration4 chapters · 32 lessons. Banks now expose payments through APIs, and regulators require it. This domain explains REST and ISO 20022 APIs, open banking under PSD2 and PSD3, the integration patterns that connect core banking to partners, and how payment APIs are designed and secured. Domain 11: Operations, exceptions and reconciliation6 chapters · 43 lessons. Most payments go through cleanly. The work of operations is everything else: the exceptions, the investigations, the reconciliation that proves it all, and the testing that keeps it safe. This domain is that work. Domain 12: Risk, compliance and regulation7 chapters · 52 lessons. A payment must be legal, safe and compliant before it moves. This domain covers sanctions screening, anti-money-laundering, fraud prevention, payment security and the regulations that bind a payment, including PSD2, PSD3 and DORA. Domain 13: Payments product and business2 chapters · 14 lessons. Payments is also a business. This domain covers how a payments product is positioned, priced and measured, how a firm joins a scheme or enters a new market, and how to decide whether to build or buy. Domain 14: Business analysis and professional practice5 chapters · 36 lessons. This domain is the working practice of a payments business analyst: gathering requirements, writing user stories and acceptance criteria, mapping messages, designing tests, and delivering a migration or a product. Domain 15: Advanced and emerging payments3 chapters · 23 lessons. Payments keep changing. This domain covers central bank digital currencies, stablecoins, AI in fraud detection and operations, embedded finance, and what richer ISO 20022 data makes possible.
