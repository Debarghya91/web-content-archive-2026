Pricing

Lessons on this website are free to read. The products below are the courses you can buy: each product gives 1 year of access in the app, and renews for a further year at half its sale price. Buy any product directly with UPI on the payment page, or in the app through the app store.

How pricing works

Each product shows two prices. The struck-through price is the regular price. The price beside it is the launch offer sale price, and it is the price a buyer will pay. Every product gives 1 year of access from purchase. Renewal adds a further year at half the sale price.

Buying everything separately costs Rs. 8,385. The Integrated Course costs Rs. 2,999 and includes everything, which saves 64 percent.

The FIRSTLAUNCH25 launch code gives 25 percent off the sale price on courses and standalone products when it is accepted at checkout. It does not apply to the Integrated Course, it does not apply to UPI payment, and it never combines with a referral code. A referral code earns the referrer Rs. 100 credit, only on Practice Experiences, Interview Ready, and the Integrated Course, and only when the referred purchase completes. A referral code gives the buyer no discount.

Free samples and free content

Every product offers 2 free samples, with a prompt to buy after the second. Interview Ready keeps 5 free questions. Chapter and lesson outlines stay free to browse, and the first lesson of every chapter stays free to read. The free situations are ABH-01 and JEB-C01, and the weekly free case stays free for everyone. The ISO 20022 Explorer is free for everyone, and the placement test stays free.

Completion records and certificates

The certificate rule is still being finalised. Completion records and certificate details will be published here.
