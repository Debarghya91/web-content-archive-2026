Pricing

The first lesson of every chapter is free to read in full, and every other lesson shows what to expect from the lesson, with the option to buy its course. The products below are the courses you can buy: each product gives 1 year of access on this website and in the app, and renews for a further year at half its sale price. Buy any product with Cashfree or PayPal on the payment page, or in the app through the app store.

How pricing works

Each product shows two prices. The struck-through price is the regular price. The price beside it is the launch offer sale price, and it is the price a buyer will pay. Every product gives 1 year of access from purchase. Renewal adds a further year at half the sale price.

Buying everything separately costs Rs. 8,389. The Integrated Course costs Rs. 2,999 and includes everything, which saves 64 percent.

The FIRSTLAUNCH25 launch code gives 25 percent off the sale price on courses and standalone products when it is accepted at checkout. It does not apply to the Integrated Course.

Every product offers 2 free samples, with a prompt to buy after the second. Interview Ready keeps 5 free questions. The Test Series keeps its first two Business Analyst tests free.
