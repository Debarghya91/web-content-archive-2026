Payment Investigation Lab

Reading about a payment is not the same as investigating one. Each exercise below drops you into a real problem, with the transaction details, the people involved and the messages on file. You trace the payment, choose the next action at each decision point, and find out whether your choice helped or made it worse. There is no single right answer to memorise. There is a correct order of checks, and a wrong order costs money.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, nothing is stored except a small note of which exercises you have finished, and no payment is made.

How to use

  1. Pick an investigation below. Read the scenario, the transaction details, the participants and the messages involved before you start.
  2. Click "Start the investigation". You will face three decision points. At each one, choose the next action. A correct first choice scores a point. A wrong choice explains why it is wrong, and you pick again.
  3. Watch the investigation path at the top of the exercise. It shows the checks in the right order, and each correct choice ticks one off.
  4. After the third decision point you get the full picture: the root cause, the resolution with its accounting and reconciliation impact, the customer impact, and the preventive controls.
  5. Work all six. A payment professional needs all six skills: telling a decision from money moved, catching a duplicate before it settles, holding for screening without overriding it, chasing a recall to its answer, respecting a cut-off, and checking the real nostro balance before promising settlement.

Golden rules, from the lessons: a camt.029 is a decision, it does not move money. Only a pacs.004 moves money back. Never refund before the funds are received and reconciled. Never re-send a rejected payment unchanged. Never override a sanctions screening hold. A cut-off is a wall, not a suggestion. The mirror nostro holds no funds.

Pick an investigation

Six investigations. Each one takes about five minutes.
