Methodology

How the Payments Training Hub course is built, how lessons are checked, and how corrections are handled.

Where the content starts

Every lesson starts from the authoritative documentation for its subject: the scheme rulebooks, the message standards, and the official guidance listed on the Sources page. That includes SWIFT and the CBPR+ usage guidelines, ISO 20022, the European Payments Council SEPA rulebooks, Pay.UK, the Federal Reserve and Nacha, NPCI, and the BIS Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures.

A lesson states what a rule or a message does, shows it with a worked example, and explains the failure modes and the operational consequences. Where schemes differ, the lesson says which scheme it is describing. Where a rule has changed, the lesson is updated to the current version.

How lessons are structured

The curriculum is 15 domains, 95 chapters and 706 lessons. Each lesson has narrated audio and an animated diagram. Around the lessons sit simulators, hands-on labs, ISO 20022 exercises, capstone projects, scenarios, interview questions and a timed test series. The structure is the same across the whole academy so a learner always knows where they are: a chapter, a lesson, an exercise, and a test.

Lessons are written for working professionals, not beginners and not exam candidates. They name the messages, the systems and the failure modes, and they work through real operational scenarios. A lesson that cannot be checked against a source is not published.

How lessons are validated

Content is checked against the current version of the source documents listed on the Sources page. Chapters carry a version and a last-reviewed date where that information is published on the chapter page. When a scheme publishes a new rulebook or a new version of a message standard, the affected lessons are checked and updated, and the chapter review date is updated with them.

Accuracy is also checked by sample audit: a set of lessons is read end to end against its sources and the findings are recorded. The audit findings are used to fix the lessons and to check the same concepts across the rest of the curriculum, so one error does not sit uncorrected in twenty other lessons.

What this site does not do

This site does not publish testimonials, student numbers, success rates, salary claims, or endorsements. Counts on the site (lessons, chapters, questions, simulators) are counted from the published curriculum, not estimated. No lesson claims a partnership, accreditation, or affiliation that does not exist. No lesson presents invented scheme rules, invented message fields, or invented references as if they were real.

Corrections

If you find an error, report it through the Contact page with the page address and the source that shows the correct position. Verified corrections are fixed and the review date is updated. The full corrections policy is on the Corrections Policy page.
