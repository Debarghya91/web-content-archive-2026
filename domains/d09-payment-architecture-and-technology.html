Domain 09: Payment architecture and technology

7 chapters · 53 lessons

Behind every payment sits a system that has to be fast, correct and always available. This domain explains how payment hubs, gateways and engines are built, how data flows through them, and how they are observed in production.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain the components of a payment hub and how they connect
  • Explain how a payment gateway moves money from checkout to bank settlement
  • Read a payment data model and a canonical mapping
  • Explain what is inside a payment engine and how operations monitors it
  • Describe an enterprise payment hub architecture end to end
