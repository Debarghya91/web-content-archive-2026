Domain 07: Clearing, settlement and liquidity

7 chapters · 49 lessons

Clearing sorts payments, settlement moves the money, and liquidity funds it all. This domain explains netting, the settlement models, how banks manage intraday liquidity, and the risks that settlement creates.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain clearing and netting and how they reduce the number of settlements
  • Compare RTGS, deferred net settlement and prefunded settlement models
  • Explain how banks manage intraday liquidity, queues and gridlock
  • Explain Herstatt risk and principal risk, and how PVP and CLS reduce them
  • Explain how a clearing system is chosen and what happens when settlement fails
