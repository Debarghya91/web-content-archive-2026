Domain 14: Business analysis and professional practice

10 chapters · 76 lessons

This domain is the working practice of a payments business analyst: gathering requirements, writing user stories and acceptance criteria, mapping messages, designing tests, and delivering a migration or a product.

What you will be able to do

  • Gather requirements and write user stories with acceptance criteria
  • Map a payment message field by field, including MT to MX
  • Design SIT and UAT, including negative testing and defect management
  • Run a capstone: scope, map, test and take a SWIFT MT to ISO 20022 migration live
  • Explain how large transaction banks organise their payment operations
