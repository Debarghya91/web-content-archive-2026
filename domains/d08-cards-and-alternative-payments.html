Domain 08: Cards and alternative payments

7 chapters · 52 lessons

A card payment is a chain of authorisation, clearing and settlement that involves the merchant, the acquirer, the network and the issuer. This domain follows that chain and covers wallets, recurring payments and the compliance that surrounds them.

What you will be able to do

  • Trace a card payment from the till to the bank accounts
  • Explain authorisation, clearing and settlement for cards
  • Explain 3D Secure, tokenisation and how chargebacks are resolved
  • Explain how the Visa and Mastercard networks work
  • Explain digital wallets, POS systems and recurring payments
