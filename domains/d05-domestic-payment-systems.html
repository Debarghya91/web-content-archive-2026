Domain 05: Domestic payment systems

15 chapters · 122 lessons

Every country settles payments differently. This domain covers the domestic systems of India, the United States, Europe and the United Kingdom, and the instant payment systems now running around the world.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain how UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS move money in India
  • Explain Fedwire, FedNow, ACH and CHIPS in the United States
  • Explain SEPA, T2, TIPS and the TARGET services in Europe
  • Explain Faster Payments, CHAPS and BACS in the United Kingdom
  • Compare instant payment systems around the world, including Pix, PayNow, NPP and PromptPay
