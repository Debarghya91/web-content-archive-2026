Domain 01: Foundations of money and payments

5 chapters · 34 lessons

Start here. This domain explains what a payment is, what money is, and how the pieces fit together before any message or system is introduced. It covers payment instruments, the parties in the ecosystem, paper instruments and cheques, and the types of money that change how a payment works.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain what a payment is and what makes it final
  • Tell the payment instruments apart and say when each one is used
  • Name the parties in the payment ecosystem and what each one does
  • Explain how cheques and paper instruments clear, and how the pull model differs from the push model
  • Explain the types of money and why the type changes the payment
