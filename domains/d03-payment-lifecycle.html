Domain 03: Payment lifecycle

2 chapters · 14 lessons

A payment is a journey with a beginning, a middle and an end. This domain follows it from initiation to final settlement, including the timelines for instant, batch and cross-border payments and the failure modes at each stage.

What you will be able to do

  • Trace a payment from initiation to final settlement
  • Explain the stages of the lifecycle and what happens at each one
  • Read a minute-by-minute timeline for instant, batch and cross-border payments
  • Name the failure modes at each stage and what they mean for the payment
