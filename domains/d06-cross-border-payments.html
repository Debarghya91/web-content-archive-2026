Domain 06: Cross-border payments

10 chapters · 87 lessons

When a payment crosses a border, it passes through correspondent banks, nostro accounts and several intermediaries. This domain explains how that chain works, what it costs, how it is tracked, and what happens when it breaks.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain correspondent banking and how nostro and vostro accounts work
  • Tell serial and cover payments apart and explain why the choice matters
  • Explain who pays the charges, how the exchange rate is set, and how gpi tracking works
  • Handle a cross-border exception: rejection, return, recall or investigation
  • Explain the trade finance instruments that support cross-border trade
