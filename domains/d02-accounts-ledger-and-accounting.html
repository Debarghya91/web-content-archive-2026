Domain 02: Accounts, ledger and accounting

3 chapters · 19 lessons

Every payment ends as an entry in a ledger. This domain explains the accounts a bank holds, what a balance means, how double-entry bookkeeping records a payment, and how a bank’s ledger systems keep those records consistent.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain the accounts a bank holds and what a balance means
  • Write the double-entry postings behind a payment
  • Explain how a ledger records a payment and how reconciliation proves it
  • Describe what happens when ledger systems disagree
