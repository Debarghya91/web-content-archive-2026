Domain 04: Messaging and standards

12 chapters · 97 lessons

Payments run on messages. This domain teaches the two message families that carry them, SWIFT MT and ISO 20022, field by field, and how the industry is moving from one to the other under CBPR+.

What you will be able to do

  • Read a SWIFT MT message field by field, including MT103, MT101 and the reporting messages
  • Read an ISO 20022 message, including pacs.008, pain.001 and pain.002
  • Explain how MT and MX messages map to each other under CBPR+
  • Explain what happens to data in translation, including truncation
  • Find a missing payment and run a claim or enquiry to closure
