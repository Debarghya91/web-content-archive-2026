Domain 15: Advanced and emerging payments

3 chapters · 23 lessons

Payments keep changing. This domain covers central bank digital currencies, stablecoins, AI in fraud detection and operations, embedded finance, and what richer ISO 20022 data makes possible.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain CBDCs and stablecoins and how they differ from bank money
  • Explain where AI is used in payments, including fraud detection and operations
  • Explain embedded finance and banking as a service
  • Explain what richer ISO 20022 data makes possible for analytics
