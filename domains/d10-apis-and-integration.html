Domain 10: APIs and integration

4 chapters · 32 lessons

Banks now expose payments through APIs, and regulators require it. This domain explains REST and ISO 20022 APIs, open banking under PSD2 and PSD3, the integration patterns that connect core banking to partners, and how payment APIs are designed and secured.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain REST APIs, ISO 20022 APIs and open banking
  • Explain PSD2 and PSD3 and what they require of banks
  • Describe the integration patterns that connect core banking, ledgers and gateways
  • Explain how a payment API is designed, versioned and secured
