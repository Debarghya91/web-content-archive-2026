Home / Payment Exceptions / SC-11

Missing RMA authorisation

Status: Illustrative scenario.

At a glance

  • Scenario: SC-11
  • Payment type: Payment blocked by missing RMA
  • Route: United Kingdom to Australia, SWIFT
  • From: Coral Supplies Pty Ltd via Meridian Bank London
  • To: Pacific Rim Bank Sydney
  • Amount: AUD 29,000
  • Messages: MT103
  • Systems: SWIFT, Correspondent banking

Exception reason

RMA block overridden and the message rejected by the network

Root cause

An operator overrode the RMA block and sent the MT103 without authorisation, so the network rejected the message and it was never delivered.

Recovery action

Operations withdraw the override, request the RMA from Harbour Bank, confirm it is granted, and resend the MT103.

What happens, step by step

  1. Coral Supplies instructs Meridian Bank London to pay AUD 29,000 to a supplier at Pacific Rim Bank Sydney.
  2. Meridian Bank creates an MT103 naming Harbour Bank Singapore as the intermediary.
  3. Meridian Bank checks RMA authorisation and finds it holds no RMA to send MT103 to Harbour Bank.
  4. The payment is blocked before sending, and operations are alerted with the RMA reason.
  5. Operations request an RMA authorisation from Harbour Bank through the SWIFT RMA service.
  6. Harbour Bank grants the RMA authorisation for MT103 messages from Meridian Bank.
  7. Meridian Bank releases the MT103 to Harbour Bank, which forwards it to Pacific Rim Bank.
  8. Pacific Rim Bank credits the supplier and Meridian Bank reconciles the nostro debit.

Accounting

  • Dr Coral Supplies customer account AUD 29,000, Cr Meridian Bank nostro at Harbour Bank AUD 29,000
  • Dr Meridian Bank nostro at Harbour Bank AUD 29,000, Cr Pacific Rim Bank nostro at Harbour Bank AUD 29,000
  • Dr Pacific Rim Bank nostro at Harbour Bank AUD 29,000, Cr Supplier account at Pacific Rim Bank AUD 29,000

What goes wrong

Coral Supplies instructs the same AUD 29,000 payment, but operations override the RMA block and try to send the MT103 without authorisation, so the SWIFT network rejects the message and returns an error. The payment is not delivered, the supplier is not paid, and Meridian Bank must withdraw the override, obtain the RMA properly, and resend the MT103 through Harbour Bank Singapore.

Business rules

  • A bank must hold a valid RMA from the receiver before it can send a payment message to that bank.
  • A payment blocked for missing RMA must not be sent until the authorisation is granted.
  • The RMA request must state the message type, the sender and the receiver for audit.

Validation rules

  • The RMA status must be checked for the receiver BIC before an MT103 is released.
  • The RMA must cover the MT103 message type for the payment to be sent.
  • The payment must be held, not rejected, while the RMA request is pending.

Acceptance criteria

  • A payment without a valid RMA is blocked before sending and the operator is alerted.
  • An RMA request is sent, granted, and the payment is released only after the grant.
  • The reconciliation report shows the nostro debit matched after the payment is resent.

Reconciliation outcome

The nostro debit matches the intermediary statement, the beneficiary credit matches the MT103 amount, and the payment closes after resending with the RMA in place.

UAT cases

  1. Send a payment without RMA and confirm it is blocked before sending.
  2. Request the RMA, confirm the grant, and release the payment.
  3. Confirm the beneficiary credit and the reconciliation match after release.
