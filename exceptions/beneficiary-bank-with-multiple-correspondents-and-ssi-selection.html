Home / Payment Exceptions / SC-06

Beneficiary bank with multiple correspondents and SSI selection

Status: Illustrative scenario.

At a glance

  • Scenario: SC-06
  • Payment type: SSI selection for a beneficiary bank
  • Route: United Kingdom to Australia, SWIFT
  • From: Coral Supplies Pty Ltd via Meridian Bank London
  • To: Pacific Rim Bank Sydney
  • Amount: AUD 64,000
  • Messages: MT103
  • Systems: SWIFT, Correspondent banking

Exception reason

Standing settlement instruction expired for the beneficiary bank

Root cause

The SSI for Pacific Rim Bank had expired, so the bank used an old correspondent that no longer held an AUD account and the payment was rejected.

Recovery action

Operations obtain the updated SSI from Pacific Rim Bank, correct the routing, and resend the payment through Harbour Bank Singapore.

What happens, step by step

  1. Coral Supplies instructs Meridian Bank London to pay AUD 64,000 to a supplier at Pacific Rim Bank Sydney.
  2. Meridian Bank looks up Pacific Rim Bank in its correspondent directory and finds two correspondents.
  3. Meridian Bank checks the standing settlement instruction for Pacific Rim Bank for AUD payments.
  4. The SSI names Harbour Bank Singapore as the preferred correspondent for AUD settlement.
  5. Meridian Bank sends the MT103 through Harbour Bank Singapore as the intermediary.
  6. Harbour Bank forwards the MT103 to Pacific Rim Bank Sydney and settles the funds.
  7. Pacific Rim Bank credits the supplier on the value date and confirms receipt.
  8. Meridian Bank reconciles the nostro debit and closes the payment with no break.

Accounting

  • Dr Coral Supplies customer account AUD 64,000, Cr Meridian Bank nostro at Harbour Bank AUD 64,000
  • Dr Meridian Bank nostro at Harbour Bank AUD 64,000, Cr Pacific Rim Bank nostro at Harbour Bank AUD 64,000
  • Dr Pacific Rim Bank nostro at Harbour Bank AUD 64,000, Cr Supplier account at Pacific Rim Bank AUD 64,000

What goes wrong

Coral Supplies instructs the same AUD 64,000 payment, but the standing settlement instruction for Pacific Rim Bank has expired, so Meridian Bank uses an old correspondent that no longer holds an AUD account. The payment is rejected by the intermediary, the supplier is not paid on the value date, and Meridian Bank must obtain the updated SSI, correct the routing, and resend the payment through Harbour Bank Singapore.

Business rules

  • The standing settlement instruction must be used to select the correspondent for a beneficiary bank with multiple correspondents.
  • The SSI must be checked for currency, value date and any expiry before the payment is sent.
  • The operator must be able to override the SSI only with approval and a recorded reason.

Validation rules

  • The beneficiary bank must have a valid SSI for the payment currency before release.
  • The selected correspondent must hold a nostro account with sufficient funds.
  • The MT103 field 57A must name the beneficiary bank and field 56A must name the intermediary.

Acceptance criteria

  • A valid SSI is selected automatically for the beneficiary bank and currency.
  • An expired SSI blocks release and warns the operator before the payment is sent.
  • The reconciliation report shows the nostro debit matched to the selected correspondent.

Reconciliation outcome

The nostro debit matches the new correspondent statement, the beneficiary credit matches the MT103 amount, and the payment closes after resending.

UAT cases

  1. Send a payment with a valid SSI and confirm the preferred correspondent is selected.
  2. Send a payment with an expired SSI and confirm release is blocked with a warning.
  3. Update the SSI, resend, and confirm the beneficiary credit and reconciliation match.
