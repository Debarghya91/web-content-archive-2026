Payments Training

Payments Training Hub teaches payments the way banks actually process them: what a payment is, how it moves, what messages carry it, where it can fail, and how a business analyst, product owner or engineer works on it. The training is one structured course of 99 chapters and 874 narrated lessons, organised into five groups, with simulators, real cases and timed tests alongside. The first lesson of every chapter is free to read in full, so you can judge the teaching before you buy anything.

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

The five groups

  • Group 1: Payments Foundations (21 chapters · 186 lessons · 14 tests). What payments are, the instruments, the lifecycle, and the domestic systems of India, the US, Europe and the UK.
  • Group 2: Messages and Cross-Border Payments (22 chapters · 220 lessons · 18 tests). SWIFT MT and ISO 20022 messages, mapping, CBPR+, and how cross-border payments move through correspondent banks.
  • Group 3: Clearing, Settlement and Exceptions (24 chapters · 208 lessons · 20 tests). Clearing, settlement, liquidity, reconciliation, exceptions and investigations, and the controls that keep payments safe.
  • Group 4: Cards, Architecture and Engineering (19 chapters · 155 lessons · 17 tests). Cards, payment hubs, gateways, APIs, architecture, testing and security: the engineering side of payments.
  • Group 5: The BA at Work and Emerging Payments (13 chapters · 105 lessons · 33 tests). The BA at work: requirements, workshops, product ownership, and what is coming next, including AI in payments.

Buy one group and you unlock that group's lessons and tests. Buy any two groups and you unlock all 105 tests. The Integrated Course unlocks everything.

Start here

  • What is a Payment (7 lessons). What a payment is, and why moving money is harder than it looks.
  • Payment Instruments and Types (7 lessons). Cards, transfers, direct debits, cheques and wallets: the instruments a BA must tell apart.
  • The Payment Ecosystem (8 lessons). Banks, schemes, processors and regulators: who does what in a payment.
  • Payment Lifecycle End to End (9 lessons). Initiation to reconciliation: the seven stages every payment passes through.

Who this training is for

  • Business analysts moving into payments, or going deeper after a first project.
  • Product owners and product managers building payment features.
  • Operations, reconciliation and investigations staff who need the why behind the work.
  • Engineers and testers who build and verify payment systems.
  • Career switchers preparing for payments interviews at banks and fintechs.

Tools and practice

Run a payment yourself in the 10 payments simulators, from lifecycle to exceptions. Work 564 real payment cases in the Investigation Experience. Test yourself with 105 timed tests and 500 interview questions. Look up 27 MT and ISO 20022 messages field by field in the Message Explorer.
