For Teams

Payments Training Hub is available for teams as a pilot programme. Pricing is agreed per team, based on the number of seats and the bundles your team needs. There is no fixed team price list on this page because every pilot is scoped with the team first.

What every team tier includes

  • Multi-seat access to the same bundles, so the whole team works from the same material.
  • An admin view of progress, so a team lead can see who has completed which chapters.
  • Shared practice areas, so the team can work through the same situations and simulators together.

Team (5 seats)

Who it is for: Small business analyst pools, engineering squads and operations teams who need a shared grounding in payments.

What it includes: multi-seat access to the same bundles, an admin view of progress, and shared practice areas.

To start a pilot, email support@paymentstraininghub.com and tell us your team size, the bundles you need, and the team you want to pilot with.

Department (25 seats)

Who it is for: A department or a larger business analyst pool, an engineering function, or an operations function that wants consistent payments knowledge across the group.

What it includes: multi-seat access to the same bundles, an admin view of progress, and shared practice areas.

To start a pilot, email support@paymentstraininghub.com and tell us your team size, the bundles you need, and the team you want to pilot with.

Enterprise (Unlimited seats plus a pilot)

Who it is for: Large organisations that want payments training across several teams or locations, starting with a pilot team before a wider rollout.

What it includes: multi-seat access to the same bundles, an admin view of progress, and shared practice areas.

To start a pilot, email support@paymentstraininghub.com and tell us your team size, the bundles you need, and the team you want to pilot with.
