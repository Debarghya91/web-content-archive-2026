Payment Solution Architect

Architecture for payments: hubs, gateways, integration, data, security, resilience and real-time design.

166Core lessons
21Chapters
13.0Hours of narration
5Simulators

Who this is for

A solution architect or senior engineer designing payment platforms.

Experience level: Senior engineer or architect, 8 to 15+ years. A solution architect or senior engineer designing payment platforms.

What you will be able to do

  • Design payment hubs, gateways and real-time platforms.
  • Choose integration, data, security and resilience patterns.
  • Defend an architecture to engineering and risk.

Prerequisites

The Technical Business Analyst pathway, or equivalent engineering or architecture experience in payments.

Capstone: Payment hub architecture document

Design a payment hub architecture for a bank.

Deliverable: A payment hub architecture document: the components, how a payment flows through them, the integration points, the data model, the resilience and disaster recovery design, and the trade-offs.

Completion criteria

  • Finish every core lesson in the sequence.
  • Pass the Architecture, APIs and Operations tests with 70% or more.
  • Complete the required simulator exercises.
  • Submit the payment hub architecture document.
