Payment Scenarios: A Reading Path Through 26 Cases

Twenty-six payment scenarios as a reading path over the existing curriculum. Twenty-three stops follow the SC scenario records, from serial and cover routing decisions through RMA, SSI selection, UETR tracking, instant payment timeouts, UPI failures, chargebacks and direct debit returns. Three further stops cover the standard subjects every scenario reader needs: the MT210 nostro funding advice, the OUR, SHA and BEN charge cases, and the ACK and NACK acknowledgement flows. Each stop names its scenario and points at the lessons that explain it; no scenario content is invented.

Who this is for

Working payments professionals and business analysts who want to learn through scenarios: one case at a time, each grounded in the lessons that explain it.

Experience level: Working BA, PO or SME, 3 to 10 years. A BA, product owner or SME who learns payments through worked cases.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain serial and cover routing decisions with the fields and messages involved.
  • Trace a payment through correspondents, SSI selection and RMA authorisation.
  • Handle timeouts, failures, returns, refunds and chargebacks across rails.

Prerequisites

A basic idea of what a payment is. Each stop points at the lessons that explain its subject.

Recommended sequence

Work through the chapters in this order. Within each chapter, complete the core lessons listed.

  1. MT103 Optional Fields Deep Dive (Lessons 3-3)
    Scenario SC-01: MT103 serial payment missing field 57A. What field 57a carries and when it appears.
  2. Serial vs Cover Payments (Lessons 1-5)
    Scenario SC-02: choosing serial vs cover for a USD payment. Both methods, the comparison, and when to use each.
  3. Serial vs Cover Payments (Lessons 3-3)
    Scenario SC-03: cover settlement through another clearing system. The cover flow step by step.
  4. Serial vs Cover Payments (Lessons 2-2)
    Scenario SC-04: same correspondent used by sender and beneficiary. The serial flow when the chain runs through one correspondent.
  5. MT101 in Depth (Lessons 1-2)
    Scenario SC-05: corporate MT101 initiation followed by bank-generated MT103 and MT202 COV. What MT101 is and how it is structured.
  6. SWIFT MT Messages (Lessons 18-18)
    Scenario SC-06: beneficiary bank with multiple correspondents and SSI selection. Exchanging standing settlement instructions through MT670 and MT671.
  7. Correspondent Banking and Nostro Vostro (Lessons 1-3)
    Scenario SC-07: sender does not know the beneficiary correspondent. What correspondent banking is and why banks need correspondent accounts.
  8. MT103 Optional Fields Deep Dive (Lessons 3-4)
    Scenario SC-08: MT103 containing fields 56A and 57A. The intermediary and account-with institutions, and how 52a and 57a change across the chain.
  9. SWIFT Fundamentals (Lessons 5-5)
    Scenario SC-09: forwarding through another SWIFT participant. How a BIC routes a message across SWIFTNet.
  10. SWIFT MT Messages (Lessons 11-11)
    Scenario SC-10: centralised SWIFT connectivity within a banking group. SWIFT connectivity inside a bank.
  11. SWIFT Fundamentals (Lessons 8-8)
    Scenario SC-11: missing RMA authorisation. The authorisation that lets banks exchange messages.
  12. MT103 Optional Fields Deep Dive (Lessons 3-5)
    Scenario SC-12: multiple intermediaries in SSI. Field 57a, the chain, and the sender correspondent in cover payments.
  13. Serial vs Cover Payments (Lessons 6-6)
    Scenario SC-13: field 72 differences between MT103 and MT202 COV. INDA, INGA, COVE and CLRG codes.
  14. SWIFT MT Messages (Lessons 3-3)
    Scenario SC-14: multiple cover messages and reference reconciliation. MT202 bank-to-bank cover payments.
  15. Charges, FX and gpi Tracking (Lessons 4-5)
    Scenario SC-15: UETR tracking across the full chain. SWIFT gpi and the unique tracking reference.
  16. SWIFT Fundamentals (Lessons 14-14)
    Scenario SC-16: preferred correspondent route unavailable, alternative routing. Routing restrictions and controlling where messages can go.
  17. TIPS in Depth: Instant Payments and Liquidity Transfers (Lessons 2-3)
    Scenario SC-17: TIPS instant payment timeout and expiry. Instant payment flows, messages and statuses.
  18. India: UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS (Lessons 2-3)
    Scenario SC-18: UPI payment failure and refund. UPI architecture and IMPS instant transfers.
  19. 3D Secure, Tokenization and Chargebacks (Lessons 6-7)
    Scenario SC-19: card chargeback after duplicate charging. The chargeback lifecycle, reason codes and dispute handling.
  20. SEPA Direct Debit Deep Dive (Lessons 5-5)
    Scenario SC-20: SEPA direct debit return and refund. Rejects, returns and refunds and the 8-week rule.
  21. Serial vs Cover Payments (Lessons 3-3)
    Scenario SC-21: cover arriving before the customer payment message. The cover flow and the timing of cover versus instruction.
  22. Serial vs Cover Payments (Lessons 2-3)
    Scenario SC-22: customer payment message arriving before the cover. The serial and cover flows and the timing of instruction versus funds.
  23. SWIFT Fundamentals (Lessons 4-5)
    Scenario SC-23: non-connected BIC forwarding through another SWIFT participant. The BIC code and how it routes a message.
  24. SWIFT MT Messages (Lessons 13-13)
    Nostro funding advice: MT210, notice that funds are coming. Lesson coverage for the funding-advice subject.
  25. Charges, FX and gpi Tracking (Lessons 1-2)
    Charge cases: OUR, SHA and BEN, who pays the charges, with worked examples of what the beneficiary actually receives.
  26. SWIFT Fundamentals (Lessons 9-9)
    Acknowledgement flows: ACK and NACK and what network validation checks. See also the UAK and UNK delivery lesson in the same chapter.

Capstone: Work one scenario end to end

Pick one of the 26 scenarios and trace it end to end: the messages, the debits and credits, the charges, and where the scenario can stop, with the lesson evidence for each step.

Deliverable: A written end-to-end trace of one scenario naming each bank, each message, each charge, and each failure point.

Completion criteria

  • Finish every core lesson in the sequence.
  • Pass the Cross-Border Test with 70% or more.
  • Submit the capstone scenario trace.
