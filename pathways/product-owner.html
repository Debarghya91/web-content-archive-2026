Product Owner

Product thinking for payments: the lifecycle, the product, the APIs, the emerging landscape, and how large banks actually run payments.

128Core lessons
17Chapters
10.0Hours of narration
4Simulators

Who this is for

A product owner or product manager responsible for a payments product.

Experience level: Product Owner or Manager, 3 to 10 years. A product owner or manager responsible for a payments product.

What you will be able to do

  • Own a payments product roadmap grounded in how payments really work.
  • Judge build-versus-buy, scheme and rail choices on evidence.
  • Explain the product to engineering, operations and compliance.

Prerequisites

The Beginner pathway, or equivalent working knowledge of payments.

Capstone: Product requirement document

Write a product requirement for a new payment feature.

Deliverable: A product requirement document for a new payment feature: the problem, the user, the payment flow, the messages and APIs involved, the success measures, and the risks.

Completion criteria

  • Finish every core lesson in the sequence.
  • Pass the Professional Practice, Emerging Technologies and Cards tests with 70% or more.
  • Complete the required simulator exercises.
  • Submit the product requirement document.
