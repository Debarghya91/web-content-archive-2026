Payments Tester

Testing payments properly: message validation, SIT and UAT, negative testing, exceptions, reconciliation and defect management, ending in the capstone project.

108Core lessons
14Chapters
8.4Hours of narration
3Simulators

Who this is for

A QA engineer, test analyst or business analyst who tests payments systems.

Experience level: QA or Test Analyst, 1 to 5 years. A QA engineer, test analyst or BA who tests payments, or is moving into payments testing.

What you will be able to do

  • Validate ISO 20022 and SWIFT messages against schemas and usage guidelines.
  • Design SIT and UAT cases, including negative and edge cases.
  • Test exceptions, returns, recalls and reconciliation, and raise defects that engineering can act on.

Prerequisites

The Beginner pathway, or equivalent working knowledge of payments.
