Payments SME

Deep expertise: clearing, settlement, liquidity, risk, TARGET services, SEPA and cards, ending in synthesis.

223Core lessons
31Chapters

Who this is for

A senior BA, product owner or operations lead who needs deep, reliable payments knowledge.

Experience level: Senior expert, 10 to 15+ years. Deep expertise for a senior BA, product owner or operations lead. Built to stay valuable at 15 years of experience.

What you will be able to do

  • Reason about clearing, settlement, liquidity and risk at field level.
  • Challenge a hub, gateway or scheme design on its real trade-offs.
  • Advise on exceptions, claims and cross-border edge cases.

Prerequisites

The Business Analyst or Technical Business Analyst pathway.

Capstone: Settlement model recommendation

Compare settlement models and recommend one for a bank.

Deliverable: A written settlement model comparison and recommendation for a bank: RTGS, deferred net settlement and prefunded models compared on liquidity, risk and cost, with a recommendation and the reasoning behind it.

Completion criteria

  • Finish every core lesson in the sequence.
  • Pass the Clearing, Rails, Cross-Border and Operations tests with 70% or more.
  • Complete the required simulator exercises.
  • Submit the settlement model recommendation.
