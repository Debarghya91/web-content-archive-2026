Sources and Review Policy

The authoritative sources the curriculum is written from, and how content is kept current.

Authoritative sources

  • SWIFT, including the CBPR+ usage guidelines for ISO 20022 migration and the MT message standards.
  • ISO 20022, the global message standard, including the pacs, pain, camt and head message definitions.
  • European Payments Council (EPC), the SEPA rulebooks for credit transfer, direct debit and instant payments.
  • Pay.UK, the operator of Faster Payments, BACS and CHAPS in the United Kingdom.
  • Federal Reserve, for Fedwire and FedNow, and Nacha for ACH in the United States.
  • NPCI, for UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS and NACH in India.
  • BIS Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI), for payment system principles and cross-border payments guidance.

Scheme operators, central banks and standards bodies are named as sources because their documentation is the authority for the subject. Naming a source is not a claim of affiliation or endorsement.

Review policy

Content is reviewed against the current version of the source documents listed above. When a scheme publishes a new rulebook or a new version of a message standard, the affected lessons are checked and updated, and the chapter review date is updated with them. Chapters carry a version and a last-reviewed date where that information is published on the chapter page.

Scheme rules change. A lesson reflects the rules as they stood at its last review date. Before relying on a rule for a live payment, check the current rulebook from the source listed above.

Corrections

Corrections reported by readers are checked against the source documents and fixed when verified. Report an error through the Contact page.
